Chapter 01

The guides

The guide text was prepared for this site using the references linked in each chapter, including Thonky, Zelda Dungeon and Zelda’s Palace. These are independent editorial guides, not official Nintendo publications.

Chapter 02

A Link to the Past maps

Ian Albert assembled and annotated the maps and explicitly permits their reuse on his website. We retain his attribution and source links. The original game graphics belong to Nintendo; the author’s permission does not grant Nintendo’s rights.

Chapter 03

Original section illustrations

Original decorative illustrations created for Hyrule Atlas. These are not game screenshots or community submissions.

Chapter 04

Original living atlas illustration

An original decorative illustration created for Hyrule Atlas. It imagines a kingdom rising from an atlas and a twilight world below. It is not a game screenshot or a navigable game map.

Chapter 06

Triforce model

A beveled Triforce modeled by PantheraLeo1359531 and released under CC0 on Wikimedia Commons. The mesh was converted to glTF for this site; Zelda and the Triforce remain associated with Nintendo.

Chapter 07

Music and free play

Sound is synthesized in your browser at A4 = 440 Hz. No game recording, MIDI file or preloaded Nintendo melody is used.

Chapter 08

Ocarina model and fingering

Updated 12 Hole Ocarina by RobSoundtrack, via Wikimedia Commons, CC BY 4.0. Based on an original design by nimaid. Converted to glTF with a blue ceramic material for Hyrule Atlas.

The five fingerings were checked against STL Ocarina’s 12-hole Bass C chart and holding manual. The illustrative mesh has not been acoustically calibrated.

Chapter 09

Credits and licenses

The locally hosted Manrope, Marcellus and IBM Plex Mono fonts are distributed with their SIL Open Font License notices. Hyrule Atlas is an independent fan project and is not affiliated with Nintendo.

Chapter 10

Corrections and removal requests

Use the private request form to report an error or request removal. Include the page address, the material concerned and the basis of your request. Your message goes to the team privately; do not include passwords or highly sensitive documents.

Chapter 11

Your progress

Guides, checklists and notes work without an account and stay in this browser. Use the backup tools before changing browser or domain. A verified account is needed only to join the community or submit art.

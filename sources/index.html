How the archive knows what it knows

Every claim should leave a trail. This page explains how the archive decides what belongs in Confirmed, Observed or Speculation.

Record 01

Confirmed

Confirmed records come from Nintendo product pages, Nintendo videos or another direct primary source. We paraphrase the information and link the original page so readers can inspect it.

Record 02

Observed

Observed records describe something visibly present in a cited trailer or clip. An observation can identify a location, color, material, motion or interface element. It cannot prove an unseen feature or a developer's intent.

Record 03

Speculation

Speculation is a clearly labeled question, theory or comparison. It never appears as news, and it moves to Confirmed only when a direct source supports it.

Record 04

Corrections and independence

When a source changes, the related record is rechecked and its verification date is updated. Material errors should be corrected openly rather than hidden.

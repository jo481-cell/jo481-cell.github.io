Source ledger

How the archive knows what it knows

Every claim should leave a trail. This page explains how the archive decides what belongs in Confirmed, Observed or Speculation.

Chapter 01

Confirmed

Confirmed records come from Nintendo product pages, Nintendo videos or another direct primary source. We paraphrase the information and link the original page so readers can inspect it.

Chapter 02

Observed

Observed records describe something visibly present in a cited trailer or clip. An observation can identify a location, color, material, motion or interface element. It cannot prove an unseen feature or a developer's intent.

Chapter 03

Speculation

Speculation is a clearly labeled question, theory or comparison. It never appears as news, and it moves to Confirmed only when a direct source supports it.

Chapter 04

Artwork and imagery

The archive presents Nintendo's Ocarina of Time artwork and screenshots from its official US and UK product pages. The original Ocarina of Time instrument illustration is preserved by Zelda Dungeon. Nintendo retains the rights to the game imagery and marks.

The October 1 environment stills are frames from Nintendo footage relayed by Perfectly Nintendo, an independent news channel. They are credited separately from Nintendo's own trailer. The image source register below records each original source and the files used by the archive.

Chapter 05

The music room

The music room presents seven fan arrangements of Ocarina of Time themes composed by Koji Kondo. Orchestra mode uses locally rendered sampled instruments; N64 MIDI mode synthesizes the source sequences in your browser. Neither mode is an official soundtrack recording. Playback starts only when you press play.

The source sequences come from Zelda Dungeon’s MIDI archive. Arrangement credits: Lost Woods / Saria’s Song, Michi; Zora’s Domain, Shawn Overn; Gerudo Valley, Jon Berney; Kakariko Village, Cameron Floyd; Windmill Hut / Song of Storms, Talek R. Julian; Meet Zelda Again, Aivi (Sirius). The Title Theme arrangement is not credited in the source. Nintendo and the respective creators retain their rights.

The orchestral adaptations use Sonatina Symphonic Orchestra, originally created by Mattias Westlund and maintained by Peter Eastman and contributors, rendered with sfizz. The production register records the instruments, adaptations and final audio files. Sample-library licensing is separate from the rights to the compositions and source arrangements.

Browser MIDI playback uses WebAudio TinySynth by Tatsuya Shinyagaito (g200kg), under the Apache 2.0 license. Source records, file fingerprints and the synthesizer license are linked below.

Chapter 06

Corrections and independence

When a source changes, the related record is rechecked and its verification date is updated. Material errors should be corrected openly rather than hidden.

Hero of Time Archive is an independent, non-commercial fan project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Nintendo.

The songs we carried.

These are the six-note trigger phrases from the original Nintendo 64 game. Use the blue A button and the four C-button directions. This songbook checks the order of the notes, so you can learn at your own pace.

A fan-made instrument, rooted in the original. The N64 controls map to D4, F4, A4, B4 and D5. The sound here is a softly synthesized wind voice, rather than the game’s original sound sample. The artwork and original melodies belong to Nintendo; the songs were composed by Koji Kondo. These are classic N64 patterns, not a claim about controls in the new game.

Button mappings and phrases checked against the Ocarina of Time decompilation. Further reading: Zelda’s Palace song reference and Dan Bruno’s study of the five-note melodies.

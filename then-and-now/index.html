Then & Now · The visual archive

Time changes the details. Some things stay.

Three familiar compositions, seen again. Take a quiet look at the silhouettes, expressions and small details that carry Hyrule from one generation to another.

Authentic Nintendo promotional artwork. Platform labels matter: these are art comparisons, not gameplay benchmarks.

Image credits & provenance ↗

Study 01 · Young Link & Navi

The first friend

The seated silhouette carries the memory. Notice the change in the fabric, the carved stump and the way the light meets Navi’s wings.

The hand positions differ. These are related promotional compositions, not aligned gameplay frames. Source & original artwork ↗

Study 02 · Adult Link

A familiar resolve

The diagonal sword still leads the eye. The newer artwork gives the tunic and leather a more tactile surface while keeping the forward-driving pose.

Compare costume and silhouette. Artwork cannot establish in-game resolution, frame rate or performance. Source & original artwork ↗

Study 03 · Link & Epona

Beyond the forest

A rider, a raised foreleg and the promise of an open field. Look at the mane, tack and saddle before returning to the energy of the whole pose.

The earlier image in this pair is from Ocarina of Time 3D, not the Nintendo 64 release. Source & original artwork ↗

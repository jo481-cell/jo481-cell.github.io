Before you set out

Your journey, at your pace.

Original base-game campaign. This route does not require DLC, glitches, or amiibo rewards; Master Mode changes combat pressure.

  • Cook ingredients together for healing and a useful effect; mixed effects can cancel.
  • Upgrade armor at Great Fairy fountains.
  • Cold, desert heat, and volcanic fire require different protection.

From the beginning to the final encounter

A path through the story.

A suggested four-Divine-Beast story route. These objectives can be reordered, and the game allows an earlier castle attempt. This is not a shrine or Korok completion guide.

Avoid Waterblight's spear in phase one; break its ice attacks and shoot its eye when platforms shrink.

Rito Village and Vah Medoh

0 / 3 steps+

Reclaim the sky.

The encounter

Use updrafts for clear shots at Windblight's eye, then attack when it falls.

A useful detail

Release the bow between shots to save stamina while airborne.

05

Goron City and Vah Rudania

0 / 3 steps+

Climb Death Mountain safely.

The encounter

Damage Fireblight at close range; throw a Remote Bomb into its vacuum attack to break the fiery shield.

A useful detail

Ordinary heat-resistant meals do not stop volcanic ignition.

Gerudo Town and Vah Naboris

0 / 3 steps+

Return the Thunder Helm.

The encounter

Block Thunderblight's rushes; during the metal-pillar phase, lift a pillar beside it with Magnesis to redirect lightning.

A useful detail

Rain and electricity make equipped metal especially dangerous.

08

Hyrule Castle

0 / 3 steps+

End the Calamity.

The encounter

Use precise dodges, parries, or Urbosa's Fury to breach the guarded phase. Shoot Dark Beast Ganon's marked weak points, then use an updraft for the final eye.

Freed Divine Beasts weaken Calamity Ganon before your duel.

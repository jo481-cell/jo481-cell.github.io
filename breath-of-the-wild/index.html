2017 / A kingdom without a path

Breath of the Wild

A distant tower. A mountain you might climb. A world that lets your curiosity lead. Start your journey through this version of Hyrule here.

← All gamesBegin on the Great PlateauChoose a direction, then allow a detourPreparation is part of the adventureMaps & detailed guides
01

Begin on the Great Plateau

Follow the opening objectives, activate the tower and explore the four shrines. Their rune abilities introduce the ways you can interact with the world. Finish the Old Man’s task to receive the paraglider before setting out across Hyrule.

  • Learn each rune before looking for a more elaborate answer to a puzzle.
  • Cook and prepare protection before entering cold areas.
  • Mark interesting places with the Sheikah Slate so you can return later.
02

Choose a direction, then allow a detour

The route toward Kakariko gives the adventure a useful thread to follow after the plateau. Towers reveal the region; shrines become useful destinations as you explore. There is room to take a different road when a ruin or a distant light catches your eye.

  • Keep an eye on your stamina before a climb or a long glide.
  • A stable is a useful place to rest, cook and find a horse.
  • Save before experimenting with a risky route.
03

Preparation is part of the adventure

Weapons wear out, the weather matters and a meal can change your chances in a fight. Carry alternatives, read the effects of ingredients and equipment, and let the environment do some of the work. You do not need to win every encounter immediately.

  • Keep food for recovery and situational effects.
  • Avoid exposed metal equipment during lightning storms.
  • Return to a difficult enemy with better equipment and a plan.

Before you set out

Your journey, at your pace.

Original base-game campaign. This route does not require DLC, glitches, or amiibo rewards; Master Mode changes combat pressure.

  • Cook ingredients together for healing and a useful effect; mixed effects can cancel.
  • Upgrade armor at Great Fairy fountains.
  • Cold, desert heat, and volcanic fire require different protection.

A path through the story.

Beyond the ending

Your route to 100%.

A 100% map is an exploration counter. Inventory, quests and optional challenges need their own audit.

This is a collection roadmap with editable counters and links to detailed locations. The bar measures completed checklist goals, not a percentage read from your game save.

Wii U, Switch or Switch 2 Edition base content.

  1. Activate towers and finish regional storylines while recording Koroks.
  2. Clear shrine and side quests, then sweep one map region at a time.
  3. After the ending, use the map counter to find missing named locations and discoveries.
Before you move on

The base game has 120 shrines and 900 Korok Seeds. DLC shrines are additional; do not add them to the base target.

Learn her song, beat Ingo twice and escape Lon Lon Ranch with Epona.

5 locations shown

  1. Learn her song as a child

    After waking Talon, speak to Malon among the ranch’s horses. Show your ocarina and repeat Epona’s Song.

    References: Shacknews · Epona

  2. Choose Epona as an adult

    Visit the ranch by day and pay Ingo to ride. Play Epona’s Song in the paddock. Mount Epona before speaking to him again.

    References: Shacknews · EponaRespec · Alex Donaldson · tested remake route

  3. Win the first race

    Challenge Ingo while mounted. Start pressing the gallop button before the race begins, take the inside line and use carrots to accelerate.

    References: Shacknews · Epona

  4. Win again and escape

    Beat Ingo a second time. When he locks the gate, approach the low eastern fence at a gallop and jump it.

    References: Shacknews · Epona

  5. Bring a cow home

    After Talon returns to the ranch, ask Malon about the obstacle course. Finish in under 40 seconds for a cow at Link’s Kokiri home. Play Epona’s Song beside it with an empty bottle for milk.

    References: Respec · Alex Donaldson · tested remake route

Find the twelve fixed songs, restore the Scarecrow Song and use the remake’s ocarina controls.

15 locations shown

  1. Accept Saria’s instrument

    Leave Kokiri Forest after Gohma; Saria gives you the Fairy Ocarina on the bridge.

    References: Respec · Alex Donaldson · tested remake route

  2. Zelda’s Lullaby

    Meet Zelda, then learn the royal melody from Impa in the castle courtyard.

    References: ZeldaCentral · remake songbook reference

  3. Sun’s Song

    Play the Lullaby before the Royal Family’s Tomb in Kakariko Graveyard. Read the tablet inside.

    References: ZeldaCentral · remake songbook reference

  4. Epona’s Song

    Show your ocarina to Malon in Lon Lon Ranch’s paddock.

    References: ZeldaCentral · remake songbook referenceShacknews · Epona

  5. Saria’s Song

    Follow the Lost Woods music to Saria in the Sacred Forest Meadow.

    References: ZeldaCentral · remake songbook reference

  6. Song of Time

    Retrieve Zelda’s ocarina from the castle moat; learn the melody in her message.

    References: ZeldaCentral · remake songbook reference

  7. Song of Storms

    As an adult, show your ocarina to the windmill musician in Kakariko.

    References: ZeldaCentral · remake songbook reference

  8. Minuet of Forest

    Meet Sheik in the adult Sacred Forest Meadow before the Forest Temple.

    References: ZeldaCentral · remake songbook reference

  9. Prelude of Light

    After the Forest Temple, return to Sheik in the Temple of Time.

    References: ZeldaCentral · remake songbook referenceRespec · Alex Donaldson · tested remake route

  10. Bolero of Fire

    Reach Death Mountain Crater through adult Goron City and meet Sheik.

    References: ZeldaCentral · remake songbook reference

  11. Serenade of Water

    Learn Sheik’s melody after obtaining the Iron Boots in the Ice Cavern.

    References: ZeldaCentral · remake songbook reference

  12. Nocturne of Shadow

    Return to Kakariko after the Water Temple and meet Sheik.

    References: ZeldaCentral · remake songbook referenceRespec · Alex Donaldson · tested remake route

  13. Requiem of Spirit

    Cross the desert to the Colossus and approach the Spirit Temple to meet Sheik.

    References: ZeldaCentral · remake songbook referenceRespec · Alex Donaldson · tested remake route

  14. Restore your Scarecrow Song

    Teach Bonooru a memorable tune at Lake Hylia as a child. Replay it to him as an adult. Use it at supported spots to summon a scarecrow for the Hookshot.

    References: ZeldaCentral · remake songbook reference

  15. Choose buttons or microphone

    Follow the notes in the ocarina’s songbook. You can also hum or play a melody Link knows into the Switch 2 microphone.

    References: Nintendo · official Switch 2 game overviewNintendo · ocarina microphone and rematches

Sources and edition changes

Switch 2 edition · sources checked November 5, 2026

This guide follows the 2026 Nintendo Switch 2 remake. Routes were checked against edition-specific walkthroughs and Nintendo documentation. We have not personally completed a console playthrough.

The N64 guide and its saved progress remain separate. Checklist totals measure this guide, not the game's completion percentage. New features are documented where the available evidence supports them.

Nintendo update notes: version 1.1.1 ↗

Changes to keep in mind

  • The remake supports microphone input as well as buttons.
  • Use the in-game staff for the current controller prompts. The Scarecrow Song is your own tune, separate from the twelve fixed melodies.

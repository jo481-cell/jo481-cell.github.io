All 100 Gold Skulltula tokens

A regional checklist for the Switch 2 remake

All Switch 2 chaptersNintendo Switch 2 · 2026 remakeBoth ages

Before you begin

  • Use the era and equipment stated on each entry; not every token is available on the first visit.
  • For soil entries, carry a bug in a bottle; Magic Beans serve different later routes.
  • Several high tokens need the Boomerang, Hookshot or Longshot; Scarecrow routes also need the song prepared across both ages.

What you will gain

100 recorded token locations; milestone rewards are claimed from the cursed family in Kakariko.

Changes to keep in mind

  • The remake's map records regional discoveries and can show the locations already found; launch reporting describes fuller regional totals after the ending.
  • The Echo Gem is the remake's nearby-Skulltula aid. Equip it when retracing a region.
  • Goron City's central-rock token is a separate adult revisit; include it when returning to the city.
  • A check means you marked this token collected. It does not read or synchronise the console save.
  • These locations were checked against documentary guides explicitly covering the 2026 Switch 2 remake. We have not personally tested all 100 on a console.
  • RPG Site and Respec are related publications; matching their routes is a documentary cross-check, not independent playtesting.
  • Entries are grouped by region. Their gs identifiers are stable HyruleAtlas checklist identifiers, not official map numbers.
  • The tree above Lake Hylia and the laboratory tank each contain a separate token; collecting one does not replace the other.
  • Time is stated where the inspected sources specify it. If an outdoor spider is absent, revisit at night and compare the game's regional counter.

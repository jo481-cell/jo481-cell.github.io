Complete the adult trading chain with the remake’s longer delivery timers.

10 locations shown

  1. Get the Pocket Egg

    Speak to Kakariko’s Cucco Lady as an adult. Let her Pocket Egg hatch; the Sun’s Song advances time.

    References: Shacknews · Biggoron trading quest

  2. Wake Talon and collect Cojiro

    Use the Pocket Cucco beside sleeping Talon in Kakariko. Free Epona and speak to Talon again if he has not gone back to the ranch. Trade the happy Cucco to its owner for Cojiro.

    References: Shacknews · Biggoron trading quest

  3. Start the mushroom delivery

    Enter the Lost Woods from Kokiri and find the clearing with tree stumps near the entrance. Show Cojiro to the craftsman’s son by a stump for the Odd Mushroom. You have four minutes and cannot use ocarina warps.

    References: Shacknews · Biggoron trading questRespec · Alex Donaldson · tested remake route

  4. Reach Granny

    Ride to Kakariko. Go through the ordinary Potion Shop’s rear exit to reach Granny’s shop. Trade the mushroom for the Odd Poultice.

    References: Shacknews · Biggoron trading quest

  5. Return to the woods

    Bring the poultice to the same clearing. Give it to the Kokiri standing there for the Poacher’s Saw.

    References: Shacknews · Biggoron trading quest

  6. Find the master craftsman

    Jump the broken bridge in Gerudo Valley on Epona. Trade the saw to the craftsman for the Broken Goron’s Sword.

    References: Shacknews · Biggoron trading quest

  7. Visit Biggoron

    Show the broken sword to Biggoron at Death Mountain’s summit. Take his Prescription to the thawed King Zora.

    References: Shacknews · Biggoron trading quest

  8. Deliver the Eyeball Frog

    King Zora gives you the frog. Reach Lake Hylia Laboratory within 2:30 via the Domain’s underwater lake passage. Do not use ocarina warps.

    References: Respec · Alex Donaldson · tested remake route

  9. Rush back to the summit

    Take the Eye Drops to Biggoron within 3:30. Ride through Hyrule Field to Kakariko, then climb Death Mountain. The Longshot can save time on the upper grates. Do not warp.

    References: Respec · Alex Donaldson · tested remake route

  10. Redeem the receipt

    Give Biggoron the drops. Wait three days, then present his receipt for the sword. The Sun’s Song can speed up the wait.

    References: Shacknews · Biggoron trading questRespec · Alex Donaldson · tested remake route

Sources and edition changes

Switch 2 edition · sources checked November 5, 2026

This guide follows the 2026 Nintendo Switch 2 remake. Routes were checked against edition-specific walkthroughs and Nintendo documentation. We have not personally completed a console playthrough.

The N64 guide and its saved progress remain separate. Checklist totals measure this guide, not the game's completion percentage. New features are documented where the available evidence supports them.

Nintendo update notes: version 1.1.1 ↗

Changes to keep in mind

  • Remake delivery limits: mushroom 4:00; frog 2:30; drops 3:30.
  • The river and Death Mountain Magic Beans are optional shortcuts.
  • The sword is two-handed, so you cannot use a shield while wielding it.

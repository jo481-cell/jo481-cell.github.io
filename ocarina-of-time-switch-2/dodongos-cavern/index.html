Use Bomb Flowers and eye switches to get the Bomb Bag, defeat King Dodongo and unlock Din’s Fire.

  1. Open the cavern

    Use the entrance Bomb Flower to destroy the cracked wall. In the main lava hall, cross to the west side and bomb the northwestern alcove for the map. Cross to the east side; bomb the southern alcove to open the route onward. The northern eastern alcove contains a Gossip Stone.

    References: Respec · Alex Donaldson · tested Switch 2 walkthrough

  2. Clear the eastern loop

    Pass the Baby Dodongos and stay clear after striking them: they explode. Push the statue onto the floor switch. Defeat both Lizalfos. In the next passages, use Bomb Flowers against the Dodongos, then carry a burning Deku Stick to the unlit torches. Return to the main hall and press the switch that opens the western route.

    References: Respec · Alex Donaldson · tested Switch 2 walkthrough

  3. Drop the enormous staircase

    Cross the lobby to the newly opened west door. In the room lined with Bomb Flowers, place one flower in the empty gap between the two rows. The simultaneous chain explosion lowers the staircase. Climb it.

    References: Respec · Alex Donaldson · tested Switch 2 walkthrough

  4. Cross above the lobby

    At the top of the staircase, clear or avoid the Armos statues and move the one blocking the ladder. Climb to the switch. Cross the high bridge above the main hall, then continue to the block-and-platform room.

    References: Respec · Alex Donaldson · tested Switch 2 walkthrough

  5. Break the high wall

    Pull the block beneath the ladder and climb to the platforms. From the Bomb Flower ledge, time a throw so the flower blasts the high cracked wall. Climb through the opening.

    References: Respec · Alex Donaldson · tested Switch 2 walkthrough

  6. Switch off the platform flames

    Shoot the eye above the far door to cross the first flame platform. Defeat the next pair of Lizalfos. In the following room, shoot the first eye, jump to the middle platform and shoot the newly visible side eye to complete the crossing.

    References: Respec · Alex Donaldson · tested Switch 2 walkthrough

  7. Collect the Bomb Bag

    Continue over the upper blade-trap room to the large chest. The Bomb Bag lets you carry and place your own bombs. Step on the nearby switch to extend the lobby elevator.

    References: Respec · Alex Donaldson · tested Switch 2 walkthrough

  8. Light the skull's eyes

    Stand on the bridge above the giant stone skull. Drop one bomb through each of the two openings so it detonates inside an eye socket. When both eyes glow, the mouth opens. Descend and enter it.

    References: Respec · Alex Donaldson · tested Switch 2 walkthrough

  9. Unlock the last door

    From the skull’s mouth, follow the block-puzzle passage. Use the lower block as a foothold to push the upper block down, then move it to reach and shift the block obstructing the route. Drop the final block into its floor slot to open the way to King Dodongo. Continue to the boss chamber. In the final chamber, bomb the cracked floor to open the drop into the arena.

    References: Respec · Alex Donaldson · tested Switch 2 walkthroughNintenderos · Dodongo Cavern remake guide

  10. Claim the second Spiritual Stone

    Drop into King Dodongo's arena. After the battle, take the Heart Container and blue warp. Darunia gives you Goron's Ruby outside.

    References: Respec · Alex Donaldson · tested Switch 2 walkthrough

  11. Visit the mountain Great Fairy

    Climb Death Mountain, using bombs on the rocks that block the way. Shelter beneath the Hylian Shield when rocks fall and climb to the summit. Bomb the cave beside the owl. Play Zelda’s Lullaby at the fountain and make the offering to unlock the magic meter and Din’s Fire.

    References: Respec · Alex Donaldson · tested Switch 2 walkthrough

  12. Visit the castle fairy

    Return to the Hyrule Castle approach. Climb past the gate by the vines and bomb the cracked wall nearby. Crawl into the fountain, play Zelda’s Lullaby and pay 50 Rupees. This fairy gives the enhanced Spin Attack; Din’s Fire came from the mountain summit.

    References: Respec · Alex Donaldson · tested Switch 2 walkthrough

King Dodongo

When his mouth opens to inhale, throw a bomb or Bomb Flower inside. Move in and strike while he is stunned.

Retreat as he recovers and keep clear of his charge or roll. Stay on solid ground and out of the lava; repeat the bomb-and-sword opening.

Sources and edition changes

Switch 2 edition · sources checked November 5, 2026

This guide follows the 2026 Nintendo Switch 2 remake. Routes were checked against edition-specific walkthroughs and Nintendo documentation. We have not personally completed a console playthrough.

The N64 guide and its saved progress remain separate. Checklist totals measure this guide, not the game's completion percentage. New features are documented where the available evidence supports them.

Nintendo update notes: version 1.1.1 ↗

Changes to keep in mind

  • The Death Mountain summit fairy now grants Din’s Fire and the magic meter.
  • The Hyrule Castle fairy grants the enhanced Spin Attack for a 50-Rupee offering.
  • Equip the Hylian Shield before the fiery rooms.
  • Dungeon collectible locations and later revisits are tracked separately from this story route.

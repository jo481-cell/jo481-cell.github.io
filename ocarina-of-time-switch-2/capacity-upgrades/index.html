Collect the inventory upgrades using the remake’s prizes and limits.

12 locations shown

  1. Adult’s Wallet: 500 Rupees

    Reach ten Skulltula tokens and claim the House of Skulltula reward in Kakariko.

    References: ZeldaCentral · remake capacity upgrades

  2. Giant’s Wallet: 1,000 Rupees

    Return to the same family at thirty tokens. Claim any earlier outstanding rewards first.

    References: ZeldaCentral · remake capacity upgradesRPG Site · remake completion route

  3. Carry 40 seeds

    As a child, score 10/10 in Castle Town’s Shooting Gallery.

    References: ZeldaCentral · remake capacity upgrades

  4. Carry 50 seeds

    Turn right at the Lost Woods entrance. Hit the target’s center three times.

    References: RPG Site · remake completion routeRespec · Alex Donaldson · tested remake route

  5. Carry 30 bombs

    Stop the rolling Goron with a bomb beside his challenge sign in Goron City, then speak to him.

    References: ZeldaCentral · remake capacity upgrades

  6. Carry 40 bombs

    Win Bombchu Bowling twice in Castle Town. The second victory awards the largest bag.

    References: ZeldaCentral · remake capacity upgrades

  7. Carry 40 arrows

    As an adult, hit all ten targets at Kakariko’s Shooting Gallery.

    References: ZeldaCentral · remake capacity upgrades

  8. Carry 50 arrows

    Bring Epona and the bow to Gerudo Fortress’s archery range. Score 1,400 to win the top quiver.

    References: ZeldaCentral · remake capacity upgradesRPG Site · remake completion route

  9. Carry 20 Deku Sticks

    Reflect the Lost Woods Business Scrub’s shots, then buy its stick upgrade for 40 Rupees.

    References: ZeldaCentral · remake capacity upgrades

  10. Carry 30 Deku Sticks

    Wear the Skull Mask for the Forest Stage Scrubs and claim their reward.

    References: RPG Site · remake completion routeRespec · Alex Donaldson · tested remake route

  11. Collect both nut upgrades

    Bomb open the Lost Woods grotto near the meadow; buy the Scrub’s upgrade for 40 Rupees. Then wear the Mask of Truth at the Forest Stage for the 40-nut bag.

    References: RPG Site · remake completion routeZeldaCentral · remake capacity upgrades

  12. Win the Golden Scale

    As an adult, fish at Lake Hylia. Aim for a catch at least 65 cm long and present it to the keeper.

    References: Respec · Alex Donaldson · tested remake route

Sources and edition changes

Switch 2 edition · sources checked November 5, 2026

This guide follows the 2026 Nintendo Switch 2 remake. Routes were checked against edition-specific walkthroughs and Nintendo documentation. We have not personally completed a console playthrough.

The N64 guide and its saved progress remain separate. Checklist totals measure this guide, not the game's completion percentage. New features are documented where the available evidence supports them.

Nintendo update notes: version 1.1.1 ↗

Changes to keep in mind

  • Wallet capacities are 200, 500 and 1,000 Rupees.
  • The top horseback-archery quiver requires 1,400 points in this remake.
  • The Silver Scale comes from the Zora diving game. The Happy Mask chapter explains how to reach the Forest Stage.
  • Heart Pieces and Skulltula locations have their own checklists.

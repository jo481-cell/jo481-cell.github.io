Free the Gorons, solve the remake’s weighted fire switch and reach Volvagia.

  1. Speak to the young Goron

    In adult Goron City, stop the rolling Goron with a bomb. Speak to him about the dragon and the Gorons until he gives you the Goron Tunic. Equip it. Pull the statue in Darunia's room to reveal the crater passage; Hookshot across the broken bridge, learn the Bolero of Fire from Sheik and descend into the temple.

    References: Respec · tested Switch 2 route

  2. Meet Darunia and free a prisoner

    In the lobby, climb the stairs and enter the left door. After speaking to Darunia, use the platforms on the left to reach a cell switch. Free the Goron and open his chest for a Small Key. Return to the lobby and unlock the right door.

    References: Polygon · Johnny Yu · Fire Temple Dungeon

  3. Search both ends of the lava chamber

    From the bridge, explore the left-side door for a second prisoner and key. On the opposite side, bomb the cracked wall to find another cell and key. Return to the bridge and unlock the far door.

    References: Polygon · Johnny Yu · Fire Temple Dungeon

  4. Ride the block above the fire

    Unlock the eastern route and climb the mesh walls. Push the stone block onto the fire jet, stand on it and ride upward. In the Torch Slug room, pull the next block out, toggle the crystal and climb. Shoot the crystal from the upper steps to reopen the climb.

    References: Polygon · Johnny Yu · Fire Temple Dungeon

  5. Search the lower rolling-orb maze

    Wait for each rolling orb to pass before crossing its track. The short left branch has the Map. Follow the right-hand perimeter to free the next Goron and take its key. Bomb the nearby cracked side wall only if you want its optional token.

    References: Polygon · Johnny Yu · Fire Temple Dungeon

  6. Open the eye-door branch

    Use a key to enter the central room off the maze. Shoot the eye above the side door and take the Compass beyond it. Return, then use the other passage into the chamber of metal platforms above lava.

    References: Polygon · Johnny Yu · Fire Temple Dungeon

  7. Return above the maze

    As you step onto the grating, a wall of fire begins following you. Run to the far end, push the red block and enter the upper maze. Rescue the southern Goron for a key. Bomb the central rock pile, descend and rescue another Goron for another key.

    References: Polygon · Johnny Yu · Fire Temple Dungeon

  8. Hold down the fire-maze switch

    Return to the moving-fire chamber and unlock its middle doorway. Explore the first fire maze’s northern side for another prisoner and key. Cross the locked middle passage. Carry a loose stone cube from the northern corner back onto the floor switch; its weight shuts down the fire traps.

    References: Polygon · Johnny Yu · Fire Temple Dungeon

  9. Defeat the first Flare Dancer

    Target the dancer and Hookshot its small core out of the fire, then strike it while it flees. Repeat until it is defeated. Ride the central platform upward. In the next room, activate the crystal switch with a delayed bomb so you can climb the fence while its flames are down.

    References: Polygon · Johnny Yu · Fire Temple Dungeon

  10. Race up the rising staircase

    At the top, press the floor switch. Run up the rising spiral staircase before the route retracts and open the Megaton Hammer chest. Retry from the switch if the timer expires.

    References: Polygon · Johnny Yu · Fire Temple Dungeon

  11. Drive the platforms downward

    Hammer the circular floor plate and drop with it. Smash the decorated statue, defeat the Keese and pound the next switch to form stairs. Continue through the lower doorway and hammer the next plate. Break the black rocks to reach the Time Block.

    References: Polygon · Johnny Yu · Fire Temple Dungeon

  12. Free the Goron below

    At the Time Block, play the Song of Time to move it, cross and hammer the rusty switch. Drop to the cell it opens and collect its key. Return above and hammer the large column so it falls into the lava room where Darunia entered the boss door.

    References: Polygon · Johnny Yu · Fire Temple Dungeon

  13. Return to the lobby's ground floor

    Go back to the entrance lobby. Hammer the totem blocking the ground-floor locked door. Clear the enemy rooms, defend against the flying tiles and defeat the second Flare Dancer. Hammer the rusty switch to free the final prisoner and collect the Boss Key.

    References: Polygon · Johnny Yu · Fire Temple Dungeon

  14. Cross to Volvagia

    Return to Darunia's lava room by the lobby's upstairs left door. The lowered column now provides a route to the boss door. Win the battle, take the heart and enter the warp for the Fire Medallion.

    References: Polygon · Johnny Yu · Fire Temple Dungeon

Volvagia

Avoid the emerging head’s flame, hammer it and use the stun to land sword strikes.

During flight, keep moving away from the breath and falling-rock shadows.

Watch for the true emergence hole rather than waiting beside a single hole; repeat the hammer-and-sword opening.

Sources and edition changes

Switch 2 edition · sources checked November 5, 2026

This guide follows the 2026 Nintendo Switch 2 remake. Routes were checked against edition-specific walkthroughs and Nintendo documentation. We have not personally completed a console playthrough.

The N64 guide and its saved progress remain separate. Checklist totals measure this guide, not the game's completion percentage. New features are documented where the available evidence supports them.

Changes to keep in mind

  • The lower rolling-orb maze contains the Map; its eye-door branch contains the Compass.
  • A carried stone cube holds the fire-maze switch down.
  • Edition-specific guides checked on 5 November 2026. This is documentary verification; HyruleAtlas has not replayed these steps on a Switch 2 console.
  • The Goron Tunic removes the heat countdown; fire and lava can still hurt you.

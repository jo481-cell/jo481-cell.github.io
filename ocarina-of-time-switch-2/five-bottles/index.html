Collect the remake’s extra Potion Shop bottle and the four other reusable bottles.

8 locations shown

  1. Catch the shopkeeper’s bug

    In Castle Town’s Potion Shop, accept the bug-catching request. Use the lent bottle to catch the insect, then speak to the shopkeeper again to keep the bottle.

    References: Shacknews · all five bottlesNintendo Insider · Alex Seedhouse · bottle locations

  2. Win Talon’s game

    Wake Talon at Hyrule Castle first. At Lon Lon Ranch, enter the left building and pay 10 Rupees. Find the three Super Cuccos within 30 seconds; set aside ordinary birds you have checked. Win the milk bottle.

    References: Shacknews · all five bottlesNintendo Insider · Alex Seedhouse · bottle locations

  3. Round up the easy Cuccos

    In Kakariko, check the construction site, village entrance, northern road and bouncing wooden crate near the central buildings. Carry each bird to the Cucco Lady’s pen. Roll into the crate to release its bird.

    References: Nintendo Insider · Alex Seedhouse · bottle locations

  4. Reach the brick pile

    Keep one Cucco in your hands. From the raised platform near the construction site, glide to the bird on the bricks. Throw both down safely and return them to the pen.

    References: Nintendo Insider · Alex Seedhouse · bottle locations

  5. Cross behind the windmill fence

    Carry a Cucco to the windmill entrance and glide over the low fence. Throw the bird there and your transport bird back toward the village. Climb the ladders behind the windmill for the seventh bird.

    References: Nintendo Insider · Alex Seedhouse · bottle locations

  6. Claim the Cucco Lady’s bottle

    Return all seven Cuccos to the pen and speak to their owner. Ask her for a location hint if one is missing.

    References: Nintendo Insider · Alex Seedhouse · bottle locationsShacknews · all five bottles

  7. Keep Ruto’s letter bottle

    Win the Silver Scale in Zora’s Domain. Dive through its Lake Hylia tunnel and retrieve the bottle underwater near the exit. Show King Zora the letter to keep the bottle.

    References: Shacknews · all five bottlesNintendo Insider · Alex Seedhouse · bottle locations

  8. Sell five Big Poes

    Bring Epona, the Fairy Bow and empty bottles to Hyrule Field as an adult. Jump the purple-lit obstacles, shoot the Big Poes and bottle their spirits. Sell five to the ruined-market Poe Collector: 500 points earns the bottle.

    References: Shacknews · all five bottlesNintendo Insider · Alex Seedhouse · bottle locationsRespec · Alex Donaldson · tested remake route

Sources and edition changes

Switch 2 edition · sources checked November 5, 2026

This guide follows the 2026 Nintendo Switch 2 remake. Routes were checked against edition-specific walkthroughs and Nintendo documentation. We have not personally completed a console playthrough.

The N64 guide and its saved progress remain separate. Checklist totals measure this guide, not the game's completion percentage. New features are documented where the available evidence supports them.

Nintendo update notes: version 1.1.1 ↗

Changes to keep in mind

  • The Potion Shop request adds a bottle absent from the N64 game.
  • The Poe bottle arrives at 500 points. The 1,000-point prize is a Gold Rupee.
  • Bottle numbers are a checklist order. Keep an empty bottle ready for quest items or supplies.

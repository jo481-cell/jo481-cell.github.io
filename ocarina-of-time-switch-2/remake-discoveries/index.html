Find the Hall of Fallen Foes, challenge defeated bosses and recognize the new fairy clues.

4 locations shown

  1. Find the Hall introduction

    After first returning to childhood, head to Kakariko and speak to Kaepora Gaebora outside the village. Then sleep in a bed, such as Link’s, to enter the Hall.

    References: Shacknews · Hall of Fallen Foes

  2. Study and challenge foes

    Inspect defeated enemies’ statues and choose an available rematch. Use a silver owl statue for a boss gauntlet. Talk to Kaepora Gaebora to leave.

    References: Shacknews · Hall of Fallen FoesNintendo · official Switch 2 game overview

  3. Finish the optional gauntlets

    Health carries between fights. Spend the Rupees offered before each battle carefully on supplies. Completing the child and adult boss rushes adds trophies to Link’s bedroom.

    References: Shacknews · Hall of Fallen FoesRespec · Alex Donaldson · tested remake route

  4. Follow Navi’s fairy clues

    When Navi pauses at a faint glow, approach and accept her prompt. Read each message. Some clues only pay off when you come back in the other age.

    References: ZeldaCentral · Fairy’s Thoughts referenceNintendo · official Switch 2 game overview

Sources and edition changes

Switch 2 edition · sources checked November 5, 2026

This guide follows the 2026 Nintendo Switch 2 remake. Routes were checked against edition-specific walkthroughs and Nintendo documentation. We have not personally completed a console playthrough.

The N64 guide and its saved progress remain separate. Checklist totals measure this guide, not the game's completion percentage. New features are documented where the available evidence supports them.

  • The Hall adds rematches and gauntlets; Fairy’s Thoughts are a new kind of environmental clue.
  • This covers documented features and how to use them. It is not a complete list of new secrets, fairy messages or trophy conditions.

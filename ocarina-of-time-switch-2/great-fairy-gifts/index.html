Magic has a price this time.

Visit each fountain with the right equipment and enough Rupees for its offering.

6 locations shown

  1. Summit: magic and Din’s Fire

    As a child, bomb the fountain entrance beside the summit owl. Play Zelda’s Lullaby on the crest and offer 25 Rupees for the meter and spell.

    References: ZeldaCentral · summit fairy and magic meterRespec · Alex Donaldson · tested remake route

  2. Castle: enhanced Spin Attack

    Climb past the child castle gate and bomb the blocked fountain entrance. Play the Lullaby and offer 50 Rupees.

    References: ZeldaCentral · castle fairy and Spin AttackRespec · Alex Donaldson · tested remake route

  3. Zora’s Fountain: Farore’s Wind

    Bomb the cracked rock beyond Jabu-Jabu. Play the Lullaby and offer 100 Rupees for dungeon warp-point magic.

    References: ZeldaCentral · Zora’s Fountain fairyRespec · Alex Donaldson · tested remake route

  4. Crater: doubled magic

    As an adult, hammer the boulders blocking the fountain in Death Mountain Crater. Play the Lullaby and offer 200 Rupees.

    References: Respec · Alex Donaldson · tested remake route

  5. Colossus: Nayru’s Love

    At the Desert Colossus, bomb the wall between the palms. Enter, play the Lullaby and offer 300 Rupees for the barrier spell.

    References: Respec · Alex Donaldson · tested remake routeRPG Site · remake completion route

  6. Castle ruins: halved damage

    With the Golden Gauntlets, leave the final castle across the rainbow bridge. Lift the huge block outside and offer 500 Rupees at the fountain after the Lullaby.

    References: Respec · Alex Donaldson · tested remake route

Sources and edition changes

Switch 2 edition · sources checked November 5, 2026

This guide follows the 2026 Nintendo Switch 2 remake. Routes were checked against edition-specific walkthroughs and Nintendo documentation. We have not personally completed a console playthrough.

The N64 guide and its saved progress remain separate. Checklist totals measure this guide, not the game's completion percentage. New features are documented where the available evidence supports them.

Changes to keep in mind

  • Din’s Fire is at Death Mountain’s summit. The child castle fountain gives the enhanced Spin Attack.
  • The six offerings are 25, 50, 100, 200, 300 and 500 Rupees.
  • Temple tools and the gauntlets are recorded in the story chapters.

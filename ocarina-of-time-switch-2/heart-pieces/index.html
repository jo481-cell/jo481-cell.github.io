Every fragment, region by region

Before you begin

  • Check the era, minigame result and inventory named on each entry before travelling.
  • Plant the relevant Magic Bean as a child before taking an adult bean route.
  • The two Hyrule Field fairy-thought rewards require an adult discovery followed by a childhood return.

What you will gain

36 heart fragments recorded individually. Four fragments form one heart container; boss containers are a separate reward category.

Track each heart fragment by region. The remake adds time-travel clues and relocates rewards, so this list preserves the required era and access conditions.

36 locations shown

  1. Castle Town — Bombchu Bowling prize

    Child route, Bomb Bag: visit Bombchu Bowling and win its heart-fragment prize; the remake's entry fee is 10 rupees.

    References: RPG Site — remake heart-fragment locationsRespec — remake walkthrough

  2. Castle Town — Richard's owner

    Child route, night: speak to a white dog so it follows you, then bring it to Richard's owner until she recognises hers.

    References: RPG Site — remake heart-fragment locationsRespec — remake walkthrough

  3. Castle Town — Treasure Chest Shop

    Child route, night: complete the Treasure Chest Shop's chest sequence; use the Lenses of Truth to identify the winning choices.

    References: RPG Site — remake heart-fragment locations

  4. Death Mountain Crater — Business Scrub below the summit

    Child route, 100 rupees: enter from the summit, descend left to the Business Scrub and buy the fragment before overheating.

    References: RPG Site — remake heart-fragment locationsRPG Site — Switch 2 item routeRespec — remake walkthrough

  5. Death Mountain Crater — Southern climbing grates

    Adult route: descend carefully beside the two climbing grates in the crater's southern section to reach the lower fragment ledge.

    References: RPG Site — remake heart-fragment locationsRespec — remake walkthrough

  6. Death Mountain Crater — Warp-point bean ride

    Adult route: plant a bean beside the crater warp point as a child, return as an adult and ride the plant.

    References: RPG Site — remake heart-fragment locations

  7. Death Mountain Trail — Above Dodongo Cavern

    Adult route: ride the bean planted beside Dodongo Cavern's entrance as a child to reach the fragment above the cave.

    References: RPG Site — remake heart-fragment locations

  8. Desert Colossus — Bean-ride upper rock

    Adult route: ride the bean planted beside the Spirit Temple as a child, continuing past the token rock to the fragment.

    References: RPG Site — remake heart-fragment locationsRespec — remake walkthrough

  9. Gerudo Fortress — Highest rooftop chest

    Adult route, Hookshot: climb above the prison area, find the high rooftop chest and grapple across to open it.

    References: RPG Site — remake heart-fragment locations

  10. Gerudo Fortress — Thousand-point archery prize

    Adult route, Epona and bow: enter Horseback Archery during the day and score at least 1,000 points for the fragment.

    References: RPG Site — remake heart-fragment locationsRespec — remake walkthrough

  11. Gerudo Valley — Behind the waterfall

    Child route: reach the lower riverbank ledge with the Cucco, then follow the northern path and stairs behind the waterfall.

    References: RPG Site — remake heart-fragment locations

  12. Gerudo Valley — Ledge left of the large bridge

    Child route: take the valley Cucco and glide southwest from the suspension bridge toward the lower ledge with a chest.

    References: RPG Site — remake heart-fragment locations

  13. Goron City — Spinning central pot

    Child route: light the surrounding torches to activate the large pot, then land a bomb inside for its fragment prize.

    References: RPG Site — remake heart-fragment locations

  14. Hyrule Field — Password grotto near Castle Town

    Read the lone-tree fairy thought as an adult; as a child, give the grotto's Business Scrub its password and pay 100 rupees.

    References: RPG Site — remake heart-fragment locationsRPG Site — Switch 2 item routeRespec — remake walkthrough

  15. Hyrule Field — Time-locked canyon chest

    Read the fairy thought beside the empty chest as an adult; return as a child and open it for the fragment.

    Adult route: ride the bean planted in the graveyard as a child to reach the ledge holding the fragment.

    Child route, night: join Dampé's digging tour; when he reports something hard, pay for the extra digging to uncover the fragment.

    Adult route: repeat Dampé's race after obtaining the Hookshot and finish in under one minute for the fragment.

Sources and edition changes

Switch 2 edition · sources checked November 5, 2026

This guide follows the 2026 Nintendo Switch 2 remake. Routes were checked against edition-specific walkthroughs and Nintendo documentation. We have not personally completed a console playthrough.

The N64 guide and its saved progress remain separate. Checklist totals measure this guide, not the game's completion percentage. New features are documented where the available evidence supports them.

Nintendo update notes: version 1.1.1 ↗

Changes to keep in mind

  • Do not search the original Ice Cavern heart-fragment spot: the remake's late collection route instead includes a child Business Scrub in Death Mountain Crater.
  • Two Hyrule Field rewards depend on first reading their fairy thought as an adult; visiting the child location alone is insufficient.
  • For the frogs' second fragment, growing them is preparation: the butterfly minigame must also be completed.
  • These are edition-specific documentary instructions, not a claim that we personally collected every fragment on a console.
  • The 36-fragment total does not mean 36 full hearts or establish a verified maximum-health HUD.
  • The highest-roof chest, bean routes and two-era clues are listed separately even when they share a region.
  • For the password grotto, reports differ on left/right descriptions of the castle approach. Use the single-tree strip and its fairy thought rather than an unsupported compass direction.
  • RPG Site and Respec share editorial provenance; agreement between them is not independent gameplay evidence.
  • After the ending, compare the game's regional collection counters before concluding a missing fragment is elsewhere.
  • Stable hp identifiers belong to this checklist; they are not an official collection order.

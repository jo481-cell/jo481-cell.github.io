Symphonic Orchestra (SSO), originally created by Mattias Westlund in 2011,
with subsequent development by the contributors to peastman/sso.

Philharmonia, OLPC, The Complete K2000, ldk1609, stamperadam, Corsica_S,
davidjwoll, Satoration, Thores, Mystified, Eddie, Campbell Barton,

Beyond the ending

Your route to 100%.

Restore optional railways while delivering passengers and cargo between story dungeons.

This is a collection roadmap with editable counters and links to detailed locations. The bar measures completed checklist goals, not a percentage read from your game save.

The main adventure does not require the multiplayer battle mode.

  1. Mark stations needing a passenger or delivery before leaving a realm.
  2. Use the restored rail network to reach rabbit habitats, stamps and hidden stations.
  3. Finish the Rabbitland and stamp rewards, then audit train parts and minigames.
Before you move on

Cargo has delivery conditions. Read each request before setting off; a damaged load may not earn its Force Gem.

Before you set out

Your journey, at your pace.

GameCube, first released in Japan in 2002. Original: eight Triforce charts. Wii U HD: three charts and five direct shards.

  • Set wind before sailing or gliding.
  • Feed Fishmen to map islands.
  • Upgrade your wallet for chart fees.

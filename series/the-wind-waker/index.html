01

Outset and the Forsaken Fortress

0 / 3 steps+

Attempt Aryll's rescue.

Route and puzzle solutions

  1. 01

    Learn swordplay from Orca, rescue Tetra in the forest and board the pirate ship. In the fortress, disable the searchlights before crossing exposed walkways.

  2. 02

    Use barrels and careful timing to pass guards while unarmed. Recover the sword near the upper approach to Aryll.

  3. 03

    After arriving on Windfall, obtain a sail and free Tingle. Return to the King of Red Lions and sail east toward Dragon Roost.

02

The first pearl

0 / 3 steps+

Help the Rito on Dragon Roost.

Route and puzzle solutions

  1. 01

    Learn the Wind's Requiem, speak with the Rito and help Medli reach the upper ledge. Use water to restore the bomb flowers and open the cavern route.

  2. 02

    Inside, cool lava with water pots and use bombs for blocked passages. Rescue Medli to obtain the Grappling Hook, then swing across the large gaps.

  3. 03

    Hook Valoo's tail above Gohma to bring the ceiling down onto its shell. Once the shell breaks, pull or approach the exposed eye and strike.

03

Forbidden Woods

0 / 3 steps+

Rescue Makar for Farore's Pearl.

Route and puzzle solutions

  1. 01

    Clear the creatures from the Great Deku Tree and climb using Baba Buds. Obtain the Deku Leaf, set the wind and glide toward the Forbidden Woods.

  2. 02

    Use the leaf to operate wind devices and move suspended platforms. Cut vines with the Boomerang, marking several targets before releasing it.

  3. 03

    Cut Kalle Demos's supporting vines with the Boomerang. Run into the fallen flower to attack its core, then leave before it closes.

04

Three pearls and a sunken kingdom

0 / 3 steps+

Draw the Master Sword.

Route and puzzle solutions

  1. 01

    Follow the Greatfish Island lead back to Windfall and obtain bombs from the pirates. Return to Outset and blast the whirlpool barrier to meet Jabun.

  2. 02

    Place the three pearls on their matching Triangle Islands. In the Tower of the Gods, use the changing water level to navigate the lower rooms.

  3. 03

    Guide the statues with the Command Melody and obtain the Bow. Shoot Gohdan's hands and eyes, then throw a bomb into its mouth; descend afterwards and draw the Master Sword.

05

Return to the fortress

0 / 3 steps+

Rescue Aryll and discover the sword's weakness.

Route and puzzle solutions

  1. 01

    Return to the fortress armed with the Master Sword and bombs. Defeat Phantom Ganon by returning his energy ball, then collect the Skull Hammer.

  2. 02

    Use the hammer on pegs blocking the rescue route. Climb the flooding tower and keep moving until you reach the rooftop arena.

  3. 03

    When the Helmaroc King drives its beak into the floor, hit it with the hammer. After breaking its mask, use the sword on the exposed head.

Checkpoints

The encounter

When the bird's beak sticks in the floor, hammer its mask; slash after the armor breaks.

A useful detail

Smash barred switches with the Skull Hammer.

06

The Earth Sage

0 / 3 steps+

Restore the first half of the sword's power.

Route and puzzle solutions

  1. 01

    Learn the Ballad of Gales and obtain Fire and Ice Arrows. Freeze Fire Mountain, collect the Power Bracelets and open the Earth Temple's stone entrance.

  2. 02

    Learn the Earth God's Lyric and play it with Medli. Inside, use the Command Melody to aim her reflected light while Link operates switches and carries mirrors.

  3. 03

    Acquire the Mirror Shield and combine beams to remove light-sensitive barriers. Illuminate Jalhalla, lift and throw him into a pillar, then defeat the scattered Poes.

The encounter

Shine light on Jalhalla, lift and throw him into spikes, then defeat the scattered Poes.

A useful detail

Keep Medli near doors that require both characters.

07

The Wind Sage

0 / 3 steps+

Finish restoring the Master Sword.

Route and puzzle solutions

  1. 01

    Use a Fire Arrow to thaw Ice Ring Isle's entrance and retrieve the Iron Boots. Learn the Wind God's Aria and find Makar behind the waterfall at Forest Haven.

  2. 02

    Guide Makar to planting spots with the Command Melody. Use the Iron Boots on spring mechanisms and the Hookshot to reconnect routes when the companions are separated.

  3. 03

    Hook Molgera's exposed tongue and strike it with the sword. Keep moving on firm sand and clear smaller worms when they interfere with your aim.

The encounter

A useful detail

08

Eight pieces of courage

0 / 3 steps+

Reopen the route to Hyrule.

Route and puzzle solutions

  1. 01

    Obtain the IN-credible Chart and work through the eight Triforce locations. Prepare the required equipment and rupees before setting off on a sea-wide collection trip.

  2. 02

    On GameCube, take each Triforce Chart to Tingle for deciphering before salvaging its shard. In HD, collect five shards directly and decipher only the remaining three charts.

  3. 03

    Use the correct chart and sea square to align the boat over the salvage light. Return to the Tower of the Gods once all eight shards are assembled.

09

Ganon's Tower

0 / 3 steps+

Bring the ocean journey to its conclusion.

Route and puzzle solutions

  1. 01

    Return to Hyrule and cross the restored route to Ganon's Tower. Complete the remembered boss rooms and follow the tower's visual clues through its maze.

  2. 02

    Use Light Arrows for Phantom Ganon and cut Puppet Ganon's strings before shooting the tail jewel. Watch the tail's position during its later forms.

  3. 03

    In the final duel, create openings with Zelda's arrows. When the battle changes, reflect her arrow with the shield to stun Ganondorf, then follow the sword opening.

Chapter references
  • Once Again, Mysterious Underwater Place ↗
  • Ganon's Tower ↗

Checkpoints

The encounter

Let Zelda's Light Arrows create openings; later reflect her shot with your shield and use the prompted parry.

A useful detail

Replenish arrows and magic before the Puppet Ganon arena.

Collection field guide

Plan your final discoveries.

Use these routes alongside the edition-specific counters. The references link to individual locations and additional maps.

Sea charts, hearts and upgrades

  1. 01

    Feed the Fishman in each sea square to build the chart. Combine nearby islands, Great Fairies, Big Octos and salvage sites in one sailing route.

  2. 02

    Separate finding a Treasure Chart from salvaging its reward. Mark both stages before counting the associated heart or rupees as collected.

  3. 03

    Collect bottles and capacity upgrades before longer optional challenges. Use the edition selector because HD changes some charts and rewards.

The Nintendo Gallery and island errands

  1. 01

    Unlock the Nintendo Gallery and take usable photographs before losing access to a subject. Review the figurine checklist before boss encounters and one-time character scenes.

  2. 02

    Prepare a sailing route before collecting Forest Water for the wilted trees. The time allowance differs between the original game and HD.

  3. 03

    Complete Zunari's trading chain and the island side quests as separate objectives. Owning a decorative trade item does not mean every trade or display reward is finished.

Edition notes

GameCube uses eight deciphered Triforce Charts; Wii U HD replaces five of those chart rewards with shards directly. HD also changes sailing convenience and several collection requirements.

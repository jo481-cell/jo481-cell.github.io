Echoes of Wisdom

Save Hyrule as Zelda, remembering useful things and moving the world with Tri.

Before you set out

Your journey, at your pace.

Switch campaign using echoes, Bind, and Swordfighter Form. Other solutions often work.

  • Learn useful echoes immediately.
  • Heal with beds; prepare smoothies.
  • Bind moves targets; Follow lets them carry Zelda.

Escape and Suthorn Ruins

Learn what Tri can do.

The encounter

Pull Seismic Talus's glowing orb out with Bind, then attack it while exposed.

A useful detail

Let combat echoes occupy enemies while you move to safety.

02

Gerudo Desert

0 / 3 steps+

Earn access to the sanctum.

The encounter

Make Mogryph collide with a statue during a charge; after it falls, attack before it takes flight again.

A useful detail

Read the statue clues before placing copies.

03

Jabul Waters

0 / 3 steps+

Reunite the Zora communities.

The encounter

Avoid Vocavor's whirlpools, break its back growths, and attack while it collapses.

A useful detail

Water Blocks provide climbing and swimming routes.

04

Hyrule Castle's rift

0 / 3 steps+

Rescue the king.

The encounter

Evade spear attacks and fire bats; return the energy ball with sword timing or let strong echoes attack during recoveries.

A useful detail

Save Swordfighter energy for a clear opening instead of leaving the form active.

05

Eldin Volcano

0 / 3 steps+

Receive Din's Sanction.

The encounter

Use Bind to pull Volvagia's green necklace and create an opening; move away before the next eruption.

A useful detail

Keep a flying echo available for lava crossings.

06

Faron Wetlands

0 / 3 steps+

Receive Farore's Sanction.

The encounter

Electrify Gohma's leg crystals to knock it down; then attack the exposed eye.

A useful detail

Keep electric attacks away from water you are standing in.

07

Hebra and Lanayru Temple

0 / 3 steps+

Receive Nayru's Sanction.

The encounter

Use fire against Skorchill's icy helmet and ice against its fiery helmet; attack its body after the armor breaks.

A useful detail

A warmed room can hide a key underwater that was inaccessible beneath ice.

08

The Eternal Forest

0 / 3 steps+

Find Link.

A useful detail

Prepare before returning Link's weapons; Zelda then loses Swordfighter Form.

09

Null's Body

0 / 3 steps+

Fight alongside Link.

The encounter

Pull Null's arms with Bind so Link can sever them, defeat summoned enemies, and join Tri in removing the final core.

A useful detail

Keep replacing combat echoes when they disappear; Zelda's role remains active throughout.

Beyond the ending

Your route to 100%.

Let Tri’s expanding abilities guide your cleanup, then use the completed-save counters to find gaps.

This is a collection roadmap with editable counters and links to detailed locations. The bar measures completed checklist goals, not a percentage read from your game save.

Core adventure and collectibles; amiibo recolors are optional.

  1. Learn unfamiliar echoes immediately and mark blocked areas for later.
  2. Finish regional quests, optional rifts and Dampé’s automaton requests.
  3. After the ending, use the Great Deku Tree’s hints for missing echoes and sweep the remaining map icons.

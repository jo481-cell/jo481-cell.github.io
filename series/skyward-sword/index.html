Beyond the ending

Your route to 100%.

Pair each surface visit with a sky sweep to open newly activated Goddess Chests.

This is a collection roadmap with editable counters and links to detailed locations. The bar measures completed checklist goals, not a percentage read from your game save.

Original motion controls.

  1. Activate Goddess Cubes as equipment opens the surface.
  2. Complete Skyloft errands by day and night, then finish Batreaux’s rewards.
  3. Upgrade permanent gear and finish the Thunder Dragon challenges before Demise.

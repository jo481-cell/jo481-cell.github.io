Before you set out

Your journey, at your pace.

Original Nintendo 64 quest. The linked detailed walkthrough also follows this version; Master Quest layouts and 3DS changes are outside its scope.

  • A bottle gives you space for fairies, fish, or Blue Fire.
  • Keep the ocarina accessible: songs open routes as well as tell the story.
  • Dungeon maps show different floors; compare them before spending a Small Key.

Zelda and the remaining stones

0 / 3 steps+

Open the way to the Temple of Time.

The encounter

Bomb King Dodongo's open mouth. Against Barinade, sever its ceiling connections and clear the jellyfish with the Boomerang.

A useful detail

The letter is underwater in Lake Hylia; the Silver Scale helps you reach it.

03

Seven years later

0 / 3 steps+

Awaken the forest and fire sages.

The encounter

Return Phantom Ganon's energy shots with sword swings. Hammer Volvagia when its real head emerges.

A useful detail

Completing the Forest Temple unlocks returning to childhood through the Master Sword.

04

Water and shadow

0 / 3 steps+

Restore Lake Hylia and enter the royal tomb.

The encounter

Pull Morpha's nucleus with the Longshot. Use the Lens to see Bongo Bongo, stun both hands, then its eye.

A useful detail

Watch the space under the central Water Temple platform when it rises.

05

Across the desert

0 / 3 steps+

Awaken the final sage.

The encounter

Reflect each Twinrova witch's beam at the other; after they merge, absorb three matching attacks before releasing the shield's charge.

A useful detail

The Spirit Temple intentionally requires both ages; do not try to force the child crawlspace as an adult.

06

The last climb

0 / 3 steps+

Rescue Zelda and defeat Ganon.

The encounter

Volley Ganondorf's spell, stun him with a Light Arrow, and attack. In the final arena, circle Ganon to strike his tail; reclaim the Master Sword and use it for the finish.

A useful detail

Refill magic, arrows, and bottles before entering the final tower.

Beyond the ending

Your route to 100%.

Alternate childhood and adulthood sweeps; several rewards require actions in both eras.

This is a collection roadmap with editable counters and links to detailed locations. The bar measures completed checklist goals, not a percentage read from your game save.

Original quest.

  1. As a child, begin mask trading, plant beans and learn the Scarecrow’s Song.
  2. As an adult, finish the trade quest and revisit every region by day and night.
  3. Use dungeon maps and regional Skulltula markers before the final inventory audit.

Before you set out

Your journey, at your pace.

N64 route. The 3DS remake changes saving, swimming, and bosses; these strategies follow the original.

  • Bank rupees before resetting time.
  • Play the Song of Time backward to slow time.
  • Reset before temples when needed; songs and permanent equipment remain.

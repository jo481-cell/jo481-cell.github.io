Before you set out

Your journey, at your pace.

Original NES adventure; first released in Japan in 1987. Western release dates and some mechanics differ from the Disk System version.

  • Collect all four additional Magic Containers before seeking Thunder.
  • Learn Life and refill in towns before long crossings.
  • Take each palace's treasure before placing its crystal.

01

Parapa Palace

0 / 3 steps+

Light the way beyond the first palace.

Route and puzzle solutions

  1. 01

    Learn Shield in Rauru and gather experience on the road before entering Parapa Palace. Shield reduces damage but uses magic needed for other spells.

  2. 02

    Explore the palace's side branches for the Candle before placing the crystal. The Candle illuminates the overworld caves used later.

  3. 03

    Against Horsehead, jump and strike the head above the armour, then retreat as the mace swings. After winning, continue to the statue and place the crystal.

A useful detail

02

Towns and Death Mountain

0 / 3 steps+

Recover the Hammer.

Route and puzzle solutions

  1. 01

    Return the stolen trophy to Ruto to learn Jump. Find the mirror for the woman in Saria to gain access to Life, then use Bagu's permission to cross the river.

  2. 02

    In Death Mountain, record cave entrances and exits rather than choosing every fork at random. Refill before the final stretch to the Hammer.

  3. 03

    Use the Hammer to break overworld rocks and reach the nearby Magic Container. In Mido, obtain Fairy through the medicine quest and use Jump to reach the Downward Thrust teacher.

03

Midoro Palace

0 / 3 steps+

Pass through breakable stone.

Route and puzzle solutions

  1. 01

    Use the Hammer to reach the swamp palace. Carry enough magic for Shield and Life, and check the lower branches for the Handy Glove.

  2. 02

    The glove allows sword strikes to break dungeon blocks. Return to blocked corridors with it instead of wasting time looking for a separate switch.

  3. 03

    Helmethead's helmets detach and fire projectiles as the fight progresses. Jump to strike the vulnerable head, then move clear of the floating helmets.

Chapter references
  • Midoro Palace ↗

Checkpoints

The encounter

Strike Helmethead's heads, then the exposed body; avoid the floating heads.

A useful detail

Detours for experience are safer than repeatedly losing a nearly complete level.

04

Island Palace

0 / 3 steps+

Reach eastern Hyrule.

Route and puzzle solutions

  1. 01

    Reach the island through the cave route from the coast. Look for the Raft inside the palace before continuing to the boss.

  2. 02

    Use Downward Thrust on enemies that are dangerous to approach head-on. Save magic for Fairy where a passage or gap requires flight.

  3. 03

    Rebonack first attacks on horseback. Use downward strikes to unseat him, then fight the exposed Iron Knuckle by alternating high and low attacks around its shield.

Chapter references
  • Island Palace ↗

Checkpoints

The encounter

Downward-thrust onto Rebonack's horse, then fight his dismounted form.

A useful detail

Fairy form can bypass narrow passages but cannot fight normally.

05

Maze Palace

0 / 3 steps+

Obtain the Boots and Reflect.

Route and puzzle solutions

  1. 01

    In eastern Hyrule, help the woman in Nabooru for Fire. Reach Darunia, rescue the child from Maze Island and return for Reflect; learn Upward Thrust from the hidden teacher too.

  2. 02

    Recover the Boots in Maze Palace. They let you traverse designated shallow-water paths on the overworld rather than every sea tile.

  3. 03

    Carock cannot be defeated by ordinary sword attacks. Cast Reflect, face his projectiles and crouch with your shield toward them.

Chapter references
  • Nabooru Town ↗
  • Darunia Town ↗
  • Maze Palace ↗

Checkpoints

The encounter

Cast Reflect and return Carock's spells with your shield.

A useful detail

Darunia also offers the upward thrust through a roof-accessible house.

06

Ocean Palace

0 / 3 steps+

Get the Flute.

Route and puzzle solutions

  1. 01

    Use the Boots to find the route across the eastern sea. Search the palace for the Flute before placing the next crystal.

  2. 02

    Do not chase every optional experience bag if your lives or magic are low. Preserve enough magic for defence and healing during the boss.

  3. 03

    Gooma swings a heavy weapon while leaving his body exposed between attacks. Step in for a sword hit, then back away before the swing reaches you.

Chapter references
  • Ocean Palace ↗

Checkpoints

The encounter

Stay just beyond Gooma's mace; jump in to strike and retreat.

A useful detail

Only specific ocean paths are walkable.

07

Hidden Palace

0 / 3 steps+

Place the sixth crystal.

Route and puzzle solutions

  1. 01

    Play the Flute to remove the River Devil. In New Kasuto, obtain the final Magic Container and Spell, then use Spell in the empty part of town to reveal the Magic Key.

  2. 02

    Play the Flute at the three-rock formation to reveal Hidden Palace. Retrieve the Cross and watch for invisible floor gaps; some deliberate falls lead to new routes.

  3. 03

    Keep magic for Jump and Fairy during the descent. Against Barba, jump to strike the dragon's head as it emerges from lava, then return to safe ground.

Chapter references
  • New Kasuto ↗
  • Hidden Palace ↗

Checkpoints

The encounter

Use Jump and downward thrust against Barba's head as it rises; avoid the lava.

A useful detail

The Cross reveals otherwise invisible enemies.

08

The Great Palace

0 / 3 steps+

Claim the Triforce of Courage.

Route and puzzle solutions

  1. 01

    Confirm that all six crystals are placed and collect every Magic Container. Learn Thunder in Old Kasuto, then refill before the long road to the Great Palace.

  2. 02

    The palace is a navigation challenge as well as a combat test. Mark elevator branches and avoid spending all your magic before the final rooms.

  3. 03

    Cast Thunder to expose Thunderbird's weak point, then jump to strike its face. Dark Link follows immediately: block, change attack height and take controlled openings instead of jumping constantly.

Chapter references
  • Great Palace ↗

Checkpoints

The encounter

Cast Thunder to expose Thunderbird's face. For Shadow Link, vary sword height and punish openings instead of spending offensive magic.

A useful detail

A game over inside this palace allows a restart at its entrance.

Collection field guide

Plan your final discoveries.

Use these routes alongside the edition-specific counters. The references link to individual locations and additional maps.

Hearts, magic and techniques

  1. 01

    Collect all four extra Heart Containers and all four Magic Containers. Magic capacity is a progression requirement because the final spell depends on it.

  2. 02

    Check the spell list and both sword techniques before the Great Palace. Attack, Magic and Life can each reach level 8; raising them makes the final route more forgiving.

  3. 03

    Save one-use extra-life dolls for a difficult late-game attempt rather than collecting them automatically while passing through.

Edition notes

The route follows the western NES game. Experience rules, enemy behaviour and some layouts differ in the original Japanese Disk System release.

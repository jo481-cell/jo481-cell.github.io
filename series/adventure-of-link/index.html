Before you set out

Your journey, at your pace.

Original NES adventure; first released in Japan in 1987. Western release dates and some mechanics differ from the Disk System version.

  • Collect all four additional Magic Containers before seeking Thunder.
  • Learn Life and refill in towns before long crossings.
  • Take each palace's treasure before placing its crystal.

The checklist tracks permanent upgrades, not a stock of consumable extra lives.

  1. Visit every town as each traversal item becomes available.
  2. Collect the life and magic expansions, then finish combat training.
  3. Reach maximum experience levels and check palace rooms before the Great Palace.

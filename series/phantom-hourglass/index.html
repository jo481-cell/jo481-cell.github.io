Find Tetra.

05

The first pure metal

0 / 3 steps+

Recover Crimsonine.

The encounter

Knock Dongorongo over with Gongoron and send a Bombchu into its mouth; later feed it bombs when it inhales.

A useful detail

Station one character safely before switching.

06

The frozen island

0 / 3 steps+

Recover Azurine.

The encounter

Reflect Gleeok's fire and ice shots toward the opposite-colored head, then grapple its tongues to posts.

A useful detail

Draw grapple paths carefully: the rope itself is part of the puzzle.

07

The drowned kingdom

0 / 3 steps+

Recover Aquanine.

The encounter

Launch upward to hammer Eox's marked body points; after its armor breaks, attack the remaining head's weak point.

A useful detail

Draw or mark puzzle patterns before changing floors.

08

The Phantom Sword

0 / 3 steps+

Free the Ocean King and Tetra.

The encounter

Grapple Bellum's slime and shoot its eyes; in the possessed-Linebeck duel, stop time when the upper screen shows its back eye open.

A useful detail

Ciela's time-stopping power works only after collecting the required Phantom Sphere.

Beyond the ending

Your route to 100%.

Use sea-chart annotations to finish islands and salvage without losing track between temple visits.

This is a collection roadmap with editable counters and links to detailed locations. The bar measures completed checklist goals, not a percentage read from your game save.

Includes the original adventure; battle records are separate.

  1. Buy charts, discover islands and mark salvage spots while gathering the pure metals.
  2. Collect Spirit Gems and return to Spirit Island at each reward threshold.
  3. Finish minigames, ship collections and the Ocean King’s Temple audit before Bellum.

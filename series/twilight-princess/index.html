Twilight Princess

Follow Midna between light and twilight as swordsman and wolf.

Before you set out

Your journey, at your pace.

GameCube/Wii campaign; Wii is mirrored, so directions use landmarks. HD changes some collection requirements.

  • Use wolf senses to track scents.
  • Defeat the last two Shadow Beasts together.
  • Restock bottles and bombs in settlements.

Kakariko and the Gorons

0 / 3 steps+

Rescue the children and enter the mines.

The encounter

Shoot Fyrus's forehead, grab a chain while wearing Iron Boots, and pull him down.

A useful detail

Equip the boots on magnetic surfaces and when resisting a Goron's charge.

Collect the Clawshot for distant targets.

07

Temple of Time

0 / 3 steps+

Return a statue to its place.

The encounter

Shoot Armogohma's open eye, then command a nearby statue to crush it.

Smash obstructions with the controlled statue.

08

The city above

0 / 3 steps+

Find the last shard.

The encounter

Pull Argorok down with Iron Boots; climb the targets and reach its back's weak point.

A useful detail

Chain targets without dropping.

09

The Palace of Twilight

0 / 3 steps+

Reunite the mirror and face Zant.

The encounter

Zant revisits earlier arenas: use their corresponding tools, then punish his exhausted spinning attacks.

A useful detail

Keep moving while carrying a Sol; its guardian hand follows.

10

Hyrule Castle

0 / 3 steps+

End the occupation.

The encounter

Return possessed Zelda's energy, wrestle Beast Ganon as a wolf, keep Zelda aligned on horseback, then dodge Ganondorf's sword and counter.

A useful detail

Bring healing supplies before climbing the final staircase.

Beyond the ending

Your route to 100%.

Combine day-and-night exploration with the wolf’s senses and the final dungeon tools.

This is a collection roadmap with editable counters and links to detailed locations. The bar measures completed checklist goals, not a percentage read from your game save.

Use the map orientation matching your console.

  1. Mark golden bugs, howling stones and Poe sightings as you travel.
  2. Return with the Spinner, Ball and Chain, Dominion Rod and Double Clawshots.
  3. Finish the Cave of Ordeals and Castle Town rewards before the final battle.

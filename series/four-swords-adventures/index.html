The Legend of Zelda: Four Swords Adventures

Hyrule becomes a stage for four heroes moving as one. Formations, shared switches and parallel worlds carry this rescue through eight regions.

Before you set out

Your journey, at your pace.

2004 GameCube Hyrulean Adventure campaign. Play solo with four Links or cooperatively; this is not the separate GBA Four Swords.

  • Collect 2,000 Force Gems per stage to break its final barriers.
  • Items and hearts do not remain as permanent stage-to-stage upgrades.
  • Choose formations for simultaneous switches and attacks.

01

Whereabouts of the Wind

0 / 3 steps+

Rescue the first maiden.

Route and puzzle solutions

  1. 01

    Lake Hylia: use Wide formation to push broad blocks and Box formation for square switches. Gather Force Gems from the side caves before reaching the barrier.

  2. 02

    Cave of No Return: inspect the supplied items and use the appropriate formation for grouped targets. Keep track of cave exits that return to a different outdoor position.

  3. 03

    Hyrule Castle: restore the sword's power with enough Force Gems, then clear the eye barriers. Return Phantom Ganon's energy volley and strike during the resulting stun.

Chapter references
  • Chapter 1 – Whereabouts of the Wind ↗

Checkpoints

The encounter

Return Phantom Ganon's energy attacks, then strike while he is stunned.

A useful detail

Revisit missed gem rooms if your sword lacks power.

02

Eastern Hyrule

0 / 3 steps+

Follow the coastal investigation.

Route and puzzle solutions

  1. 01

    The Coast: carry Moon Pearls to the matching portals and compare the two versions of a blocked route. A dangerous passage can be safer on the other side.

  2. 02

    Village of the Blue Maiden: follow the residents' conversations and exchanges. Revisit the relevant houses after obtaining each requested item.

  3. 03

    Eastern Temple: use Moon Gates to bypass hazards, the Bow for red-eye targets and the Lamp for torches. Exchange tools when needed rather than expecting one item to solve the entire stage.

  • Chapter 2 – Eastern Hyrule ↗

Checkpoints

A useful detail

A blocked path can have a usable counterpart across a Moon Gate.

03

Death Mountain

0 / 3 steps+

Climb to the Tower of Flames.

Route and puzzle solutions

  1. 01

    Death Mountain Foothills: arrange the Links to cover grouped switches, then inspect the opened paths for the stage's equipment.

  2. 02

    The Mountain Path: collect the Hammer and use it on the obstacles designed for it. Separate the Links when a narrow route or distant control requires individual movement.

  3. 03

    Tower of Flames: climb through its indoor and outdoor passages. Against the Helmaroc King, break the helmet with the Hammer before attacking the exposed head.

Chapter references
  • Chapter 3 – Death Mountain ↗

Checkpoints

The encounter

Break Helmaroc King's helmet with the Hammer, then attack its exposed head.

A useful detail

Retrieve a new item when the stage changes.

04

Near the Fields

0 / 3 steps+

Infiltrate Hyrule Castle.

Route and puzzle solutions

  1. 01

    The Field: cut grass to reveal the four switches and use the group to lift large logs. Recheck side areas once the main path opens.

  2. 02

    The Swamp: take Dampé's Power Bracelet and use it to move heavy obstacles. Carry the necessary object through the route before switching away from the tool that handles it.

  3. 03

    Infiltration of Hyrule Castle: avoid the searchlights and bring the Lamp to Jalhalla. Light the four torches, attack during the opening and finish the smaller enemy revealed inside.

Chapter references
  • Chapter 4 – Near the Fields ↗

Checkpoints

The encounter

Light all four castle torches without entering Jalhalla's beam, slash him, then attack the small enemy inside.

A useful detail

Cross formation can activate four nearby targets.

05

The Dark World

0 / 3 steps+

Reach the Temple of Darkness.

Route and puzzle solutions

  1. 01

    Lost Woods: compare signs and paths between worlds and use the Lamp for dark passages. Mark portal positions to avoid repeating the same loop.

  2. 02

    Kakariko Village: search houses, back entrances, bushes and underground rooms for all ten thieves, then carry them to the pen.

  3. 03

    Temple of Darkness: coordinate switches across the connected worlds. Return Phantom Ganon's real energy volley instead of chasing every image.

Chapter references
  • Chapter 5 – The Dark World ↗

Checkpoints

The encounter

Return the real energy volley; do not chase every phantom image.

A useful detail

Keep track of which world contains the open passage.

06

The Desert of Doubt

0 / 3 steps+

Uncover the desert's route.

Route and puzzle solutions

  1. 01

    Desert of Doubt: speak with the Gerudo elder and guard, then read the wind clue before entering the maze. Follow the instructed direction relative to the wind.

  2. 02

    Desert Temple: inspect the branches for tools and keys before returning to its central obstacles. Keep enough Force Gems to empower the final barrier-breaking attack.

  3. 03

    The Pyramid: use the Link whose colour matches Big Moldorm's tail. Continue matching colours after the boss divides.

Chapter references
  • Chapter 6 – The Desert of Doubt ↗

Checkpoints

The encounter

Attack Big Moldorm's tail with the Link matching its colour, including after it divides.

A useful detail

Watch the current wind direction at each maze screen.

07

Frozen Hyrule

0 / 3 steps+

Climb the Tower of Winds.

Route and puzzle solutions

  1. 01

    Frozen Hyrule: bomb the concealed cave entrances and pull their levers to open the climb. Control movement carefully during the ice-covered fights.

  2. 02

    The Ice Temple: leave a statue on a switch that must stay pressed, and use Cross formation for the grouped floor switches. Inspect loose pots before assuming a room has no mechanism.

  3. 03

    Tower of Winds: climb the side-view rooms and coordinate separate platforms. Defeat the tower's guardian and complete Zelda's rescue before moving to the sky region.

Chapter references
  • Chapter 7 – Frozen Hyrule ↗

Checkpoints

A useful detail

Allow extra stopping distance on ice.

08

Realm of the Heavens

0 / 3 steps+

Confront Vaati.

Route and puzzle solutions

  1. 01

    Realm of the Heavens: study platform movement before committing the formation to a jump or narrow crossing. Recover isolated Links before moving to the next screen.

  2. 02

    The Dark Cloud: complete the Shadow Link encounters and recover the Dark Mirror. Match the required colours when enemies stop responding to another Link.

  3. 03

    Palace of Winds: use bombs on Vaati's tornado. In the later phase, launch above him, pass through the opening with a downward attack and strike from beneath the arena.

Chapter references
  • Chapter 8 – Realm of the Heavens ↗

Checkpoints

The encounter

Bomb the tornado; later launch above Vaati, down-thrust through his opening, and attack beneath the arena.

A useful detail

Regroup before using a cannon.

09

The escape and Ganon

0 / 3 steps+

Seal the final threat.

Route and puzzle solutions

  1. 01

    Escape with Zelda after defeating Vaati; the adventure still has a final confrontation. Keep the group together through the collapsing route.

  2. 02

    Avoid Ganon's trident and clear the enemies when transported to the Dark World. Return to attack during his recovery windows.

  3. 03

    Protect Zelda while she prepares the light orb. Return the incoming projectiles, then shoot an arrow through her orb toward Ganon.

Chapter references
  • Chapter 8 – Realm of the Heavens ↗

Checkpoints

The encounter

Return Ganon's projectiles while Zelda prepares her orb. Fire an arrow through the light orb into Ganon.

A useful detail

Use wide formation for sword coverage without standing in the trident's path.

Collection field guide

Plan your final discoveries.

Use these routes alongside the edition-specific counters. The references link to individual locations and additional maps.

Stage completion and optional rewards

  1. 01

    Aim to collect at least 2,000 Force Gems in each stage so the Four Sword can break its ending barriers. If short, explore the unopened side routes instead of repeatedly striking the barrier.

  2. 02

    Collect Heart Containers and protective upgrades for the current stage, remembering that these do not form a permanent twenty-heart inventory across the campaign.

  3. 03

    Treat Tingle's Tower activities and other multiplayer modes separately from the twenty-four story stages. Their availability depends on the mode and player setup.

Edition notes

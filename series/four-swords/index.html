Before you set out

Your journey, at your pace.

Original 2002 GBA multiplayer adventure, requiring two to four players. The 2011 Anniversary Edition adds solo control of two Links and extra areas.

  • Agree who carries each pedestal item.
  • Rupees fund revival, so protect the team's supply.
  • Bring every player to the exit before advancing.

Learn the shared controls

0 / 3 steps+

Make the party work together.

Route and puzzle solutions

  1. 01

    Practise carrying and throwing a partner before beginning a scored stage. Agree who stays on a switch and who crosses first.

  2. 02

    Inspect the available item pedestals when entering a new puzzle. Give each player a clear role instead of having everyone exchange equipment at once.

  3. 03

    Before leaving a room, recover separated partners and check accessible rewards. A coordinated exit is safer than racing ahead alone.

02

Sea of Trees

0 / 3 steps+

Earn the forest's first key.

Route and puzzle solutions

  1. 01

    Explore the forest's branches and check the ground before charging through cut vegetation. Keep a route back to the party while collecting scattered rupees.

  2. 02

    Use the supplied tools to move between isolated platforms and operate paired mechanisms together.

  3. 03

    Strike Big Manhandla's buds with the matching coloured Link. When levers appear, pull them together to expose the next targets.

03

Talus Cave

0 / 3 steps+

Complete the ice challenge.

Route and puzzle solutions

  1. 01

    Approach icy corners slowly and counter the slide before reaching an edge. Do not throw a teammate onto a platform that the receiving player has not checked.

  2. 02

    Keep the Pegasus Shoes for the boss encounter and line up each charge before running.

  3. 03

    Ram Dera Zol into the icy wall, then slash it while frozen. In the later phase, coordinate the colour information visible to the players.

04

Death Mountain

0 / 3 steps+

Claim the third opening key.

Route and puzzle solutions

  1. 01

    Wait for lava hazards to finish their cycle before crossing. Leave enough room between players so one mistake does not send the whole party into danger.

  2. 02

    Assign the distant-switch task to a player whose tool can safely reach it, then regroup before the boss.

  3. 03

    Return Gouen's projectile with the Link matching its current colour. After the boss disperses into flames, find and strike its vulnerable body.

05

Open Vaati's Palace

0 / 3 steps+

Turn three victories into the final route.

Route and puzzle solutions

  1. 01

    Finish Sea of Trees, Talus Cave and Death Mountain to earn the initial key set. Review the stage-selection screen before choosing Vaati's Palace.

  2. 02

    For higher key tiers, replay the regions with the required rupee goals. Efficient collection matters more than winning a race against teammates.

  3. 03

    Check every participating player's access before starting the palace route. Different key sets open different challenges.

06

Cross the palace

0 / 3 steps+

Reach the wind sorcerer together.

Route and puzzle solutions

  1. 01

    Read each room as a cooperative puzzle: identify the exit, the switches holding it open and the tools needed to connect them.

  2. 02

    Use the Cape for its intended gaps and control movement around fans. Avoid committing every player to a collapsing floor at once.

  3. 03

    Recover health and choose the arena's equipment before beginning Vaati. Save the party's coordination for the colour-dependent phases.

07

Vaati and the rescue

0 / 3 steps+

Release Zelda and complete the first quest.

Route and puzzle solutions

  1. 01

    Send bombs into Vaati's tornado and detonate them near him. Repeat until he drops within sword range.

  2. 02

    In the later battle, pass the changing-colour projectile between the matching Links to create a stun.

  3. 03

    Read the colours shown by the exposed arm targets and coordinate attacks on the corresponding sides. Do not assume every projectile follows the same colour rule.

Checkpoints

The encounter

Time a bomb to explode beside Vaati inside the tornado. In the colour phase, use the matching Link against projectiles and exposed coloured targets.

A useful detail

Call out the projectile's current colour; a volley can change the required player.

Collection field guide

Plan your final discoveries.

Higher keys and Anniversary bonuses

  1. 01

    Replay the three regions for the higher key sets and the expanded palace challenges. Keep the rupee goal visible while planning which optional branches to explore.

  2. 02

    In Anniversary Edition, finish the Realm of Memories for its sword reward and the Hero's Trial for its advanced technique. These areas are not part of the original GBA campaign.

Edition notes

The original Four Swords requires multiplayer; Anniversary Edition adds solo control and bonus areas. Stages select different room layouts, so the route focuses on tools, cooperation and boss rules rather than inventing a fixed room sequence.

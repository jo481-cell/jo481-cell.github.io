The Legend of Zelda: Four Swords

Four colours, shared obstacles and a competitive rupee total: cooperation opens the route even when everyone wants the biggest purse.

Before you set out

Your journey, at your pace.

Original 2002 GBA multiplayer adventure, requiring two to four players. The 2011 Anniversary Edition adds solo control of two Links and extra areas.

  • Agree who carries each pedestal item.
  • Rupees fund revival, so protect the team's supply.
  • Bring every player to the exit before advancing.

Tri Force Heroes

Three heroes share one health bar. Think in heights, positions, and complementary tools.

Before you set out

Your journey, at your pace.

3DS: solo with two Doppels or three local players; no two-player campaign. Official online play ended in April 2024.

  • A totem's top hero uses items; its bottom hero moves.
  • Switch solo heroes deliberately.
  • Match costumes to stage hazards.

01

Woodlands

0 / 3 steps+

Learn to work at three heights.

Route and puzzle solutions

  1. 01

    Deku Forest and Buzz Blob Cave: learn to throw teammates onto higher ledges and shoot raised targets from a totem. Keep the required item user at the top.

  2. 02

    Moblin Base: separate the heroes for switches and rebuild the totem after each crossing. Bring all three to the Triforce gateway.

  3. 03

    Forest Temple: shoot arrows through flames to light the torches. Bomb Margoma's opening, throw a hero onto the body and strike the eye; increase the totem's height as required.

The encounter

02

Riverside

0 / 3 steps+

Combine water pillars with the Gripshot.

Route and puzzle solutions

  1. 01

    Secret Fortress and Abyss of Agony: use Water Rod pillars as temporary stepping stones. Raise the correct hero before throwing them across the next gap.

  2. 02

    Cove of Transition: leave heroes on separate switches, open the route and reunite them in the correct order. Avoid stranding the only hero who can reach the remaining player.

  3. 03

    Water Temple: use the Gripshot to cross targets and retrieve teammates. Pull Arrghus's smaller eyes away, then use a tall Gripshot totem to reach its central eye.

Checkpoints

The encounter

Pull Arrghus's small eyes with the Gripshot and slash; later put the Gripshot hero atop a full totem to stun its main eye.

A useful detail

Allow water pillars to settle before moving a full totem.

03

Volcano

0 / 3 steps+

Cross moving platforms and minecart tracks.

Route and puzzle solutions

  1. 01

    Blazing Trail: use the Gust Jar to send teammates across gaps and the Boomerang to recover a hero left behind. Confirm a safe landing before throwing.

  2. 02

    Hinox Mine and Den of Flames: time attacks and switches from the moving carts. Return enemy bombs while keeping them away from the other heroes.

  3. 03

    Fire Temple: coordinate the timed switches before the boss. Attack Moldorm's tail, then increase the Bow user's height as its weak point rises.

Checkpoints

The encounter

Attack Moldorm's tail; as it rises, stack two and then three heroes with the Bow on top.

A useful detail

Separate heroes before handling bombs near a crowded cart.

04

Ice Cavern

0 / 3 steps+

Control slippery terrain.

Route and puzzle solutions

  1. 01

    Frozen Plateau and Snowball Ravine: use Fire Gloves to melt barriers and light torches. Let rolling snowballs block the icy vents before crossing.

  2. 02

    Silver Shrine and Ice Temple: use the Hammer on spring platforms and move the heroes in stages. A switch operator may need to join a moving platform last.

  3. 03

    Hammer Blizzagia's head until the shell breaks, then use sword attacks on the exposed jewel. Avoid following its body so closely that the turn catches every hero.

05

Fortress

0 / 3 steps+

Reach the Lady's lair.

Route and puzzle solutions

  1. 01

    Sealed Gateway: leave one hero at the necessary control while the others take the lift. Reunite only after the route stays open.

  2. 02

    Bomb Storage and Training Ground: redirect bombs toward their targets and use the correct totem height for switches. Spread out when explosives are active.

  3. 03

    The Lady's Lair: prepare for returning boss mechanics. Bomb Margoma, reach Moldorm's raised tail and remove Arrghus's protective eyes before striking its jewel.

Checkpoints

The encounter

The Lady's Pets revisit earlier bosses: bomb Margoma's opening, reach Moldorm's tail, then clear Arrghus's elemental eyes and stun its jewel.

A useful detail

Bring everyone to the exit.

06

The Dunes

0 / 3 steps+

Retrieve another part of the Lady's outfit.

Route and puzzle solutions

  1. 01

    Infinity Dunes: use Water Rod columns and hammer-operated platforms to cross the sand. Leave a hero as a counterweight when the platform requires balance.

  2. 02

    Stone Corridors and Gibdo Mausoleum: solve the room before moving every hero away from the entrance. Burn Gibdo wrappings to make the enemies vulnerable.

  3. 03

    Desert Temple: strike Stalchampion from behind, use the Hammer on the exposed heart and build a tall Boomerang totem for the final head phase.

The encounter

A useful detail

07

The Ruins

0 / 3 steps+

Read colors in the dark.

Route and puzzle solutions

  1. 01

    Illusory Mansion and Palace Noir: keep torches lit where darkness hides enemies or routes. Position a second hero safely before advancing with the active one.

  2. 02

    Lone Labyrinth: match each hero to the coloured paths. A full totem is not always the correct way through a colour-specific obstacle.

  3. 03

    Grim Temple: free trapped teammates promptly. Strike Prismantus's orbs with the matching heroes, then throw a hero onto the exposed body to attack the eye.

Chapter references
  • Illusory Mansion ↗
  • Palace Noir ↗
  • Lone Labyrinth ↗
  • Grim Temple ↗

Checkpoints

The encounter

Have each hero hit Prismantus's matching orb; throw a hero onto it to strike the eye once all three go dark.

A useful detail

Color-matched platforms require the appropriate hero, even when the destination looks close.

08

Sky Realm and Lady Maud

0 / 3 steps+

Break Princess Styla's curse.

Route and puzzle solutions

  1. 01

    Floating Garden, Deception Castle and Dragon Citadel: combine throws, totem height and the available tools. Check where every hero will finish a platform sequence before starting it.

  2. 02

    Sky Temple: prepare for a gauntlet and recover health when available. Maud removes your items, so the final fight depends on swords, positioning and totems.

  3. 03

    Throw heroes across Maud's barrier, return her energy ball and climb the colour-matched parasols. Collect the final outfit material and return to Hytopia to finish Styla's rescue.

Chapter references
  • Floating Garden ↗
  • Deception Castle ↗
  • Dragon Citadel ↗
  • Sky Temple ↗

Checkpoints

The encounter

Throw heroes over Maud's barrier and attack behind her; return her energy ball, then climb matching-color parasols to reach her head.

A useful detail

Stay spread out when a colored lightning marker singles out one hero.

Collection field guide

Plan your final discoveries.

Use these routes alongside the edition-specific counters. The references link to individual locations and additional maps.

Challenges, outfits and the Den of Trials

  1. 01

    Complete each normal stage before tackling its challenge variants. Choose an outfit that addresses the specific restriction, such as terrain, item range or survival.

  2. 02

    Check the material rewards before replaying a stage for an outfit. Normal clears, challenge clears and crafted outfits are separate collection goals.

  3. 03

    Enter the Den of Trials after its update content is available. Clear each enemy wave before advancing, conserve recovery resources and treat the trial as an endurance challenge separate from Maud's campaign.

Edition notes

Play solo with two Doppels or with three local players; the campaign has no two-player mode. Official Nintendo 3DS online play has ended. The challenge variants and Den of Trials are separate from the thirty-two normal stages.

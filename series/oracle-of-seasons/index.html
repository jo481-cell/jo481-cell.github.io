The Legend of Zelda: Oracle of Seasons

Winter builds bridges. Summer grows ladders. Learn to read Holodrum as four overlapping landscapes rather than one fixed map.

Before you set out

Your journey, at your pace.

Original 2001 Game Boy Color quest. Animal-companion terrain can vary. A linked game adds events and an additional shared finale.

  • Change seasons while standing on a tree stump.
  • Collect each new seed type when its tree becomes accessible.
  • Read the Maku Tree's guidance between dungeons.

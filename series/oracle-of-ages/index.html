Before you set out

Your journey, at your pace.

Original 2001 Game Boy Color quest. This is a standalone start; linked play changes some events and adds the shared finale.

  • Note your era before following an overworld clue.
  • Keep a supply of Mystery Seeds for possession battles.
  • The Harp's successive tunes expand where you can travel.

Beyond the ending

Your route to 100%.

The Oracle games form a linked pair. Keep passwords and ring transfers together.

This is a collection roadmap with editable counters and links to detailed locations. The bar measures completed checklist goals, not a percentage read from your game save.

62 standard rings; GBA-only shop rings are excluded.

  1. Finish one game and use its completion secret to begin the other.
  2. Return linking secrets to their original owners and carry back the reward passwords.
  3. Use the Hero’s Secret for the opposite order if pursuing every ring and event.

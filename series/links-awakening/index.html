Before you set out

Your journey, at your pace.

1993 Game Boy main quest. DX adds the optional Color Dungeon. Switch 2019 changes controls and adds Chamber Dungeons; those features are not required here.

  • On Game Boy, assign tools to A and B; sword and shield are not permanently mapped.
  • Continue the trading sequence for the Magnifying Lens; buy the Bow before the finale.
  • Learn the Ballad of the Wind Fish from Marin.

01

Tail Cave

0 / 3 steps+

Find the first instrument.

Route and puzzle solutions

  1. 01

    Take the shield from Tarin and reclaim the sword at the beach. In the forest, bring the mushroom to the witch, then use her Magic Powder on the raccoon to open the route to the Tail Key.

  2. 02

    Inside Tail Cave, collect Roc's Feather and use it to cross floor gaps. Match the symbols on the rotating enemies before collecting their key.

  3. 03

    Moldorm's vulnerable point is its tail. Stay near the middle of the platform and circle behind it; falling sends you away from the arena.

02

Bottle Grotto

0 / 3 steps+

Lift the island's obstacles.

Route and puzzle solutions

  1. 01

    After the Moblin raid, rescue BowWow from the cave and bring him to Goponga Swamp. He eats the flowers blocking Bottle Grotto.

  2. 02

    Light the entrance torches and retrieve the Power Bracelet. For the Pols Voice puzzle, defeat the rabbit-like enemy first, the bat second and the Stalfos last.

  3. 03

    Block the Genie's fireballs, strike the bottle until it stops, then lift and throw it with the bracelet. Once it breaks, attack the exposed Genie.

Chapter references

  • Bottle Grotto ↗

Checkpoints

The encounter

Dodge fire, strike the moving bottle, then lift and throw it; attack the Genie once the bottle breaks.

A useful detail

Return BowWow afterward.

03

Key Cavern

0 / 3 steps+

Acquire the Pegasus Boots.

Route and puzzle solutions

  1. 01

    Complete Richard's request for five Golden Leaves in Kanalet Castle, then cross the field behind his villa to dig up the Slime Key.

  2. 02

    Inside Key Cavern, collect the Pegasus Boots and combine running with Roc's Feather to clear longer gaps. Defeat all enemies in suspicious rooms before leaving.

  3. 03

    Dash into Slime Eye to make it drop, then strike and run through the connection between its halves. Jump when the separated eyes land to avoid being stunned.

Chapter references

  • Key Cavern ↗

Checkpoints

The encounter

Dash into the wall to drop Slime Eye, then cut and dash through its narrowing connection.

A useful detail

Long jumps use Boots and Feather together.

04

Angler's Tunnel

0 / 3 steps+

Learn to swim.

Route and puzzle solutions

  1. 01

    Bring Marin to wake the walrus blocking Yarna Desert. Defeat the Lanmola for the Angler Key, unlock the waterfall and enter from the mountain route above.

  2. 02

    Learn the order of the glowing floor tiles in the clue room, then repeat it in the matching puzzle room. Obtain the Flippers to explore deep water.

  3. 03

    The Angler Fish is fought underwater. Swim near its head, strike the glowing lure and move away from falling debris and smaller fish.

Chapter references

  • Angler's Tunnel ↗

Checkpoints

The encounter

A useful detail

Dive to explore deep water.

05

Catfish's Maw

0 / 3 steps+

Recover the Hookshot.

Route and puzzle solutions

  1. 01

    Help the ghost return to its house and grave, then reach the entrance in Martha's Bay by diving through the underwater approach.

  2. 02

    Track the recurring Master Stalfos encounters until you recover the Hookshot. Strike the skeleton to collapse it and use a bomb while it is down.

  3. 03

    For Slime Eel, pull a genuine head out of a wall opening with the Hookshot. Attack its exposed body while staying clear of the rotating tail in the centre.

The encounter

A useful detail

06

Face Shrine

0 / 3 steps+

Lift heavier statues.

Route and puzzle solutions

  1. 01

    Visit the southern shrine for the Face Key and the mural before opening Face Shrine. Bring bombs and prepare to lift the large elephant statues.

  2. 02

    Acquire the stronger Power Bracelet and throw statues at sealed doors. For chess-piece puzzles, account for the landing movement rather than throwing blindly.

  3. 03

    When Facade's face appears in the floor, place a bomb on it. Keep moving around temporary holes; avoid standing beside a hole as it opens.

Chapter references
  • Face Shrine ↗

Checkpoints

The encounter

Place bombs on Facade's face while avoiding flying tiles and pits.

A useful detail

Bombs are essential for the boss.

07

Eagle's Tower

0 / 3 steps+

Bring down the upper floor.

Route and puzzle solutions

  1. 01

    Learn the Frog's Song of Soul, revive the flying rooster and use it to reach the Bird Key. Follow the mountain caves to Eagle's Tower.

  2. 02

    Carry the iron ball through the tower and throw it into all four pillars. Plan how to move the ball across barriers before leaving it behind; destroying the pillars lowers the upper floor.

  3. 03

    Collect the Mirror Shield before climbing to Evil Eagle. Raise it against feathers and walk into the wind to stay on the platform, then strike the eagle's beak during its passes.

Chapter references
  • Tal Tal Mountains ↗
  • Eagle's Tower ↗

Checkpoints

The encounter

Block Evil Eagle's gust with the shield and attack when it approaches.

A useful detail

Plan the ball's path before dropping through holes.

08

Turtle Rock

0 / 3 steps+

Take the eighth instrument.

Route and puzzle solutions

  1. 01

    Use the Mirror Shield to pass the flamethrower cave. Play the Frog's Song of Soul outside Turtle Rock, defeat the awakened entrance guardian and enter.

  2. 02

    Steer the moving platform to fill every gap in its floor puzzle. Collect the Magic Rod, then use it to melt ice while preserving blocks needed as stepping stones.

  3. 03

    Shoot Hot Head with the Magic Rod as it emerges from lava. Continue firing at the exposed core once the outer shell breaks.

Chapter references
  • Turtle Rock ↗

Checkpoints

The encounter

Strike Hot Head with the Magic Rod as it rises from lava.

A useful detail

The rod melts ice; consider the path before firing.

09

The Wind Fish's Egg

0 / 3 steps+

Follow your own maze clue.

Route and puzzle solutions

  1. 01

    Finish the trading sequence to obtain the Magnifying Lens. Read the previously unreadable library book and record the maze directions from your own save.

  2. 02

    Play the Ballad of the Wind Fish outside the Egg with all eight instruments. Follow your recorded directions inside; another player's sequence may not match.

  3. 03

    Change tools for the Nightmare's forms: Magic Powder for the blob, reflected projectiles for the wizard, sword attacks for the familiar monsters and arrows or the boomerang for the final eye.

Chapter references
  • Wind Fish's Egg ↗
  • Trading Sequence ↗

Checkpoints

The encounter

Powder the slime, return magic, attack exposed sword forms, then shoot the final open eye while jumping its arms.

A useful detail

Use the clue from your game instead of a memorized maze route.

Collection field guide

Plan your final discoveries.

Use these routes alongside the edition-specific counters. The references link to individual locations and additional maps.

Trading, seashells and hearts

  1. 01

    Advance the trading chain whenever you enter a new settlement. It eventually gives the Magnifying Lens needed to read the Egg's route and obtain the boomerang.

  2. 02

    Revisit the beach, castle, prairie and mountain caves after gaining the shovel, bracelet, Flippers and Hookshot. Mark the individual seashell and heart locations in the reference for your edition.

  3. 03

    In the original and DX versions, visit the Seashell Mansion at the five- and ten-shell milestones before collecting more; Switch uses a different reward ladder.

Edition-specific optional adventures

  1. 01

    In DX and Switch, read the hidden library book for the Color Dungeon's graveyard clue. Complete its colour puzzles and choose a tunic reward.

  2. 02

    DX photographs include events tied to temporary companions and story situations. Check the photograph list before advancing those scenes.

  3. 03

    On Switch, return to Dampé as new dungeon chambers become available. Complete his challenges separately from the eight main instruments.

Edition notes

Game Boy, DX and Switch have different collection totals. DX adds the Color Dungeon and photographs; Switch has Chamber Dungeons, bottles and fifty seashells. Use the edition selector before tracking completion.

Before you set out

Your journey, at your pace.

1993 Game Boy main quest. DX adds the optional Color Dungeon. Switch 2019 changes controls and adds Chamber Dungeons; those features are not required here.

  • On Game Boy, assign tools to A and B; sword and shield are not permanently mapped.
  • Continue the trading sequence for the Magnifying Lens; buy the Bow before the finale.
  • Learn the Ballad of the Wind Fish from Marin.

Beyond the ending

Your route to 100%.

Choose the edition first: the island’s collectibles changed substantially on Switch.

This is a collection roadmap with editable counters and links to detailed locations. The bar measures completed checklist goals, not a percentage read from your game save.

12 heart pieces; no Color Dungeon or photo album.

  1. Advance the trading sequence while clearing the eight instrument dungeons.
  2. Revisit beaches, caves and mountain paths with the final tools.
  3. Finish the fishing, minigame and collection rewards before entering the Wind Fish’s Egg.

Death Mountain and the Magic Mirror

Your first glimpse of another Hyrule.

Original SNES route. GBA puzzles can differ, especially in the Ice Palace.

The key to this chapter

In the Dark World, stand where Spectacle Rock would be in the Light World and use the Magic Mirror. You will appear on top of the rock.

Before you set outPower Glove · Lantern

RewardsMagic Mirror and access to the Tower of Hera

Step-by-step walkthrough

  1. Find the trail
  2. Guide the old man
  3. Receive the Mirror
  4. Reach the plateau
  5. Pass into Darkness
  6. Return to the right place

  1. North of the Sanctuary, lift the stone blocking access to the mountain cave. Enter with your lantern.

  2. In the cave, accompany the old man to the exit. Avoid the holes and stay on the passable path.

  3. The old man gives you the Magic Mirror. Its refuge also allows you to recover life.

  4. Climb up the ladders and paths outside. Watch out for rocks rolling down the slope.

  5. Step onto the warp tile near Spectacle Rock. Without the Moon Pearl, Link becomes a rabbit. This is expected at this point.

  6. Use the Mirror on the location of the rock. Take the Piece of Heart, then go down to the Tower of Hera.

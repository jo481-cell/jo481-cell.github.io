Kakariko Village: bottles and early equipment

Kakariko becomes your home base.

The key to this chapter

Buy the bottle from the merchant, enter the back of the tavern and talk to the sick child to get the net.

Step-by-step walkthrough

  1. Find the sage
  2. A first bottle
  3. The tavern chest
  4. The Bug-Catching Net
  5. The first Pieces of Heart
  6. Head east

  1. Head west to Kakariko and ask about Sahasrahla. His hideout is near the eastern ruins.

  2. Keep 100 rupees for the village merchant. The bottle purchased allows you to transport a potion, a fairy or a bee.

  3. Go around the tavern to the north to use its back entrance. Open the chest in this small room for a second bottle.

  4. Talk to the sick child in his house. With the net, capture a fairy and choose an empty bottle; she will help you after a defeat.

  5. Use bombs to explore the village well and the basement of the thieves’ hideout. Mark the Pieces of Heart you collect with the map’s Hearts filter.

  6. Go to the small house southwest of Eastern Palace. Speak to Sahasrahla, then head to the palace entrance.

The key to this chapter

Free the blacksmith transformed into a frog. Use the Magic Mirror to escort him to his Light World workshop, then have your sword tempered.

Before you set outTitan's Mitt · Mirror · Hammer

RewardsTempered Sword, Flute, enhanced magic and optional tools

Step-by-step walkthrough

  1. Bringing the blacksmiths together
  2. Temper the sword
  3. Recover the grove’s music
  4. Get the powder
  5. Reduce magic cost
  6. Prepare the Turtle Rock
  7. Collect the medallions
  8. Complete defensive tools

  1. South of the Village of Outcasts, lift the dark rocks and speak to the frog. Use the Magic Mirror and escort the transformed blacksmith to the Light World workshop.

  2. Pay the blacksmiths, leave the area then return for the upgraded sword. You do not have to wait on site.

  3. Speak to the musician in the Dark World grove to receive the Shovel. Dig among the flowers in the northwest of the Light World grove to find the Flute. Play it before Kakariko’s bird statue.

  4. Collect the mushroom from the Lost Woods, give it to the witch then return to get the powder from her shop.

  5. Hammer the obstacle near the blacksmiths and drop into the well. Sprinkle Magic Powder on the altar to unlock half magic consumption.

  6. On the northeast shore of Lake Hylia in the Light World, open the hidden cave with a bomb and take the Ice Rod.

  7. Obtain Ether near Hera with the Book and Master Sword; Quake with the fish of the northeast dark lake; Bombos on the desert ledge via the Mirror.

  8. Find the Magic Cape beneath a Light World grave. The Cane of Byrna is in the spike-filled cave on Dark World Death Mountain. Bring healing supplies and enough magic.

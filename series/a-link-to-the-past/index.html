01

LIGHT WORLD · The rain, the castle and the secret passage.

A Rainy-Night Rescue

Before you set outNo items required

RewardsSword, shield, lantern and Sanctuary Heart Container

  1. Open the chest in the house to take the lantern. Get out into the rain and head to the castle to the north.

  2. In the eastern garden, lift the lone bush and drop into the hole. Talk to your uncle to receive a sword and shield, then follow the underground passage into the courtyard.

  3. Enter the castle, go down to the dungeons and collect the keys from the guards. Take the boomerang to immobilize enemies.

  4. Throw pots at the ball-and-chain guard outside Zelda’s cell, or strike between his attacks. Collect his Big Key, unlock the prison cell and speak to Zelda.

  5. Return to the throne. Push the furniture to the right; cross the dark passage. Take the key from the chest, then the key from the rat in the sewers.

  6. Push the central block of the passage upwards. In the last room, pull the lever on the right. Listen to the wise man and take the heart container.

A hint for this chapter +

The path to Zelda begins next to the guarded door.

Reveal the solution

Go around the castle to the right and lift the isolated bush. After the rescue, push the piece of furniture behind the throne from its left.

02

LIGHT WORLD · Kakariko becomes your home base.

Getting Ready in Kakariko

Before you set outZelda at the Sanctuary

RewardsNet, bottles and meeting with Sahasrahla

  1. Head west to Kakariko and ask about Sahasrahla. His hideout is near the eastern ruins.

  2. Keep 100 rupees for the village merchant. The bottle purchased allows you to transport a potion, a fairy or a bee.

  3. Go around the tavern to the north to use its back entrance. Open the chest in this small room for a second bottle.

  4. Talk to the sick child in his house. With the net, capture a fairy and choose an empty bottle; she will help you after a defeat.

  5. Use bombs to explore the village well and the basement of the thieves’ hideout. Mark the Pieces of Heart you collect with the map’s Hearts filter.

  6. Go to the small house southwest of Eastern Palace. Speak to Sahasrahla, then head to the palace entrance.

A hint for this chapter +

A well-explored village gives you several chances to survive.

Reveal the solution

Buy the bottle from the merchant, enter the back of the tavern and talk to the sick child to get the net.

03

LIGHT WORLD · First pendant · learn to aim.

The Eastern Palace

Before you set outSword · Lantern

RewardsBow, Pendant of Courage and Pegasus Boots

  1. Lift the central pot to open the door. Cross the corridor of cannonballs using the alcoves as shelter.

  2. Collect map and compass in the accessible wings. Locate the large chest in the center; you will return with the Big Key.

  3. In the east wing, go through the dark rooms. Lift the pot from the upper right corner in the skeleton room.

  4. Go to the western rooms. Defeat the enemies, including the awakened Eyegore. Lift the pot they guarded and use the floor switch to obtain the Big Key.

  5. Return to the large chest and open it. Collect arrows under the pots before going through the north door.

  6. Wake the Eyegores and attack their open eyes. Red Eyegores require arrows. Collect the dropped key and activate the switches in the final rooms.

  7. Defeat the Armos, then take the heart and the pendant. Never leave a boss room without both rewards.

  8. Return to his shelter to get the Pegasus Boots. They allow you to hit objects and reach the library book.

A hint for this chapter +

The big treasure chest is not your first destination.

Reveal the solution

Defeat the enemies in the western rooms to reach the pot surrounded by Bubbles. Lift it and step on the switch to reveal the Big Key.

THE BOSS Armos Knights ↗

Shoot when the six statues come alive. Stay back during their rounds; move aside when they charge. The last knight jumps on you: move, then shoot.

04

LIGHT WORLD · Second pendant · the strength beneath the sand.

The Desert Palace

Before you set outPegasus Boots · Book of Mudora · Lantern

RewardsPower Glove and pendant

  1. South of Kakariko, hit the library shelf with the boots. Take the Book of Mudora.

  2. In the desert to the southwest, read the central inscription with the book. Enter when the passage opens.

  3. Explore the northwest of the first set. Run into the support holding a key to make it fall.

  4. Use this key to the east; eliminate the enemies and cross the corridor of projectiles to reach the Big Key.

  5. Return to the west. The switch hidden under a pot opens the large chest room: take the glove.

  6. Exit southwest; go down the ledge for the Piece of Heart. Go back up and lift the stones in front of the north entrance.

  7. Move between the rooms of flying tiles, taking the keys under the pots. In the last room, light the four torches.

  8. Defeat the three Lanmolas, then take the heart container and the pendant.

A hint for this chapter +

The exterior exit is part of the dungeon.

Reveal the solution

Obtain the Power Glove in the west wing, step out onto the ledge and reach the northern entrance behind the white rocks.

THE BOSS Lanmolas ↗

Watch for moving sand. Strike the heads as they emerge, away from diagonal debris. The last worm throws more stones: give it space.

05

LIGHT WORLD · Your first glimpse of another Hyrule.

Climbing Death Mountain

Before you set outPower Glove · Lantern

RewardsMagic Mirror and access to the Tower of Hera

  1. North of the Sanctuary, lift the stone blocking access to the mountain cave. Enter with your lantern.

  2. In the cave, accompany the old man to the exit. Avoid the holes and stay on the passable path.

  3. The old man gives you the Magic Mirror. Its refuge also allows you to recover life.

  4. Climb up the ladders and paths outside. Watch out for rocks rolling down the slope.

  5. Step onto the warp tile near Spectacle Rock. Without the Moon Pearl, Link becomes a rabbit. This is expected at this point.

  6. Use the Mirror on the location of the rock. Take the Piece of Heart, then go down to the Tower of Hera.

A hint for this chapter +

The rabbit can still use the Mirror.

Reveal the solution

In the Dark World, stand where Spectacle Rock would be in the Light World and use the Magic Mirror. You will appear on top of the rock.

06

LIGHT WORLD · Third pendant · don't forget the Pearl.

The Tower of Hera

Before you set outMagic Mirror · Lantern

RewardsMoon Pearl and last pendant

  1. Lower the blue blocks and go down. Take the small key, then adjust the crystal so you can go back up.

  2. Collect the dungeon map. Crystal switches change the colored barriers; star switches change the floor holes.

  3. Open the northwest passage, survive the flying tiles then reach the room with four torches. Light them to reveal the Big Key.

  4. Go back up and take the upper stairs. Push the beetles away from the holes, then collect the compass.

  5. On the upper floor, use the star to open the correct hole. Fall from its northern edge and open the chest for the Moon Pearl.

  6. Take the stairs again. Keep the hearts under the pots to heal before Moldorm, then enter his arena.

  7. After victory, take heart and pendant. Also check that the Pearl is in the inventory before leaving the mountain.

A hint for this chapter +

Stars move holes, and falls can help you.

Reveal the solution

On the floor above the large chest, activate the star near the spinning flames. Approach the hole north of the bumper from its northern edge and drop down.

THE BOSS Moldorm ↗

Only hit the tip of its tail. Keep the center of the platform and avoid exchanges at the edge. A fall resets his life; return via the stairs.

07

LIGHT WORLD · The victory which opens a second adventure.

Master Sword and the Sorcerer

Before you set outThree pendants · Moon Pearl

RewardsMaster Sword and lasting access to the world of Darkness

  1. In the Lost Woods, reach the pedestal clearing. With the three pendants, remove Master Sword; the other swords in the wood are decoys.

  2. Follow Zelda's call to the Sanctuary. The rescue now continues at the top of the castle.

  3. On the upper terrace of the castle, hit the magic barrier with Master Sword and enter the tower.

  4. Go up, light the dark rooms and eliminate the guards to get the keys. Pots serve as weapons and contain resources.

  5. After the scene with Zelda, cut the curtain behind Agahnim to access his arena.

  6. Return his appropriate attacks until he is defeated. You are transported to the top of the Pyramid.

  7. If Link remains a rabbit, return to Hera for the Moon Pearl. The Mirror returns to the Light and leaves a temporary return portal.

A hint for this chapter +

The sorcerer himself provides you with your projectile.

Reveal the solution

Return his large energy balls with the sword. Stay away from fragmenting projectiles and lightning.

THE BOSS Agahnim ↗

Stand in front of him to return the big balls. When it moves to the top center to summon lightning, move off its axis. The sword does not directly harm him.

08

DARK WORLD · Crystal I · open the roads with a hammer.

The Palace of Darkness

Before you set outMoon Pearl · Bow · Bombs · 110 rupees for Kiki

RewardsHammer and first crystal

  1. From the Pyramid, cross the eastern ruins. Pay the monkey to follow you, then to open the palace; avoid the blows that scare him away.

  2. Take the basement key on the left using the switch. To the right, the teleporter leads to the Goriyas rooms and the map.

  3. Bomb the cracked walls near the map room. To hit a red Goriya, fire an arrow, then move so the enemy mirrors you into its path.

  4. Return to the central route. Drop to the lower level at the marked gaps to reach the Big Key treasure chest. Use the floor maps to follow the connections.

  5. Quickly cross the collapsing bridge. In the maze, take the key to the southeast then bomb the east wall to reach the Hammer.

  6. Flip the turtle enemies with the Hammer before striking them. Collect the compass and lower-floor keys, then return to the switches and green statue.

  7. Hold the button with the statue, shoot the green eye then take the descent. The crystals and teleporters lead to the boss.

  8. Defeat Helmasaur, take heart and crystal. The Hammer now opens many exterior passages.

A hint for this chapter +

A statue hides the last road.

Reveal the solution

After the Hammer, place a statue on the switch then aim at the eye of the green statue with an arrow to open the descent.

THE BOSS Helmasaur King ↗

Destroy his mask with a Hammer or bombs. Once the green gem is discovered, use sword or arrows. Avoid his tail when he swings it.

09

DARK WORLD · Crystal II · acting in one world changes the other.

The Swamp Palace

Before you set outHammer · Flippers · Magic Mirror

RewardsHookshot and second crystal

  1. In Zora's Domain in the northeast of the Light World, purchase the Flippers for 500 rupees. They make it possible to cross the palace.

  2. Go to the southern ruins in the Dark World. Use the Magic Mirror, enter the corresponding Light World building and pull the right lever.

  3. Exit and return to the Mirror portal. Enter the dark palace, cross the water and eliminate the enemies to reveal the first key.

  4. Go down, look for the keys under the skulls and activate the valves. The Hammer allows you to lower obstacles.

  5. From the central pool, open to the northwest and push the valve to the left. Set the crystal to lower the orange blocks, then swim into the southwest channel. To the west, push the bottom block to the left then the central block up. Go up; fall into the northeast hole and open the chest to the east.

  6. Take the Hookshot. Use the pots as attachment points to cross the gaps and reach the distant platforms.

  7. Use the Hookshot on the pot east of the big treasure chest to reach a key, then hook onto the pots to the north. Beyond the locked door, hold the switch down with the statue. Use the left doorway on the east side. Descend to the lever and drain the water. Return to the waterfalls and walk through the second one from the right. Collect the last key beneath a skull before the final door.

  8. Enter the boss room with the Hookshot ready. Take the receptacle and the crystal after the fight.

A hint for this chapter +

The dry entrance is not a definitive dead end.

Reveal the solution

Use the Magic Mirror outside Swamp Palace. Enter the corresponding Light World building, pull the right lever, then return through the portal you left outside.

THE BOSS Arrghus ↗

Isolate each small cloud with the Hookshot and destroy it with the sword. Once the body is discovered, avoid its fall then strike between its movements.

DARK WORLD · Crystal III · a dungeon scattered in the woods.

The Skull Woods

Before you set outMoon Pearl · Hammer · Hookshot · Bombs · Magic available

RewardsFire Rod and third crystal

  1. From the bridge east of the bomb shop, go back up to the broken bridge. Above, a grass arrow indicates where to use the Hookshot towards the west bank. Continue north then west, past the cemetery and the Dark Sanctuary, then reach the village and the forest to the north.

  2. Drop into the openings to collect keys, map and compass. Move as soon as a shadow of a hand appears above Link.

  3. In the middle area, move the statue onto the switch to keep the door open. Take the Big Key behind this passage.

  4. Enter the big treasure chest section from the forest. Bomb the cracked wall, then pull the lever to remove the obstacle and open the treasure chest.

  5. Go out and reach the northwest end of the woods. Burn the large skull obstruction with the Fire Rod.

  6. Use the star switches to change the floor holes and cross the room. Light the torches, cut the curtain of vines, then defeat the Gibdo carrying a key.

  7. Avoid the hands and jump into the arena. After victory, collect heart and crystal.

A hint for this chapter +

The holes in the forest are dungeon entrances.

Reveal the solution

Alternate exits and holes to go around interior walls. After the Big Key, blow up the crack leading to the lever that opens access to the large chest.

THE BOSS Mothula ↗

The Fire Rod and sword both damage Mothula. Watch the moving floor and spikes, and keep room to retreat instead of chasing every opportunity to attack.

DARK WORLD · Crystal IV · bring in the light.

Thieves’ Town

Before you set outHammer · Bombs

RewardsTitan's Mitt and fourth crystal

  1. In the center of the Village of Outcasts, pull the statue gate to reveal the entrance to the dungeon.

  2. Explore the two heights of the first rooms. Take map, compass and Big Key; gateways sometimes hide lower access points.

  3. Go through the large north door. Look for the keys under the skulls and use a bomb to activate the crystal after your passage.

  4. Upstairs, go to the room lit by a window. Throw a bomb at the crack in the middle of the light beam.

  5. Go back down to the cells, lift the big block and open the prison. Talk to the girl and take the key from the chest.

  6. In the room to the southwest, lower an obstacle with the Hammer and quickly open the large chest before the floor collapses.

  7. Go back to the room under the attic and cross the sunbeam with her. Prepare for battle.

  8. Defeat Blind and take the rewards. The Mitt now allows you to lift black rocks.

A hint for this chapter +

The prisoner must not simply be taken back outside.

Reveal the solution

Bomb the lit floor of the attic. Then bring the prisoner back into the sunlight in the room below.

THE BOSS Blind ↗

Hit the head attached to the body. After several blows, it comes off and another one appears. Avoid floating heads and focus attacks on the body.

12

LIGHT WORLD · A better equipped hero for the second half.

The Essential Detours

Before you set outTitan's Mitt · Mirror · Hammer

RewardsTempered Sword, Flute, enhanced magic and optional tools

  1. South of the Village of Outcasts, lift the dark rocks and speak to the frog. Use the Magic Mirror and escort the transformed blacksmith to the Light World workshop.

  2. Pay the blacksmiths, leave the area then return for the upgraded sword. You do not have to wait on site.

  3. Speak to the musician in the Dark World grove to receive the Shovel. Dig among the flowers in the northwest of the Light World grove to find the Flute. Play it before Kakariko’s bird statue.

  4. Collect the mushroom from the Lost Woods, give it to the witch then return to get the powder from her shop.

  5. Hammer the obstacle near the blacksmiths and drop into the well. Sprinkle Magic Powder on the altar to unlock half magic consumption.

  6. On the northeast shore of Lake Hylia in the Light World, open the hidden cave with a bomb and take the Ice Rod.

  7. Obtain Ether near Hera with the Book and Master Sword; Quake with the fish of the northeast dark lake; Bombos on the desert ledge via the Mirror.

  8. Find the Magic Cape beneath a Light World grave. The Cane of Byrna is in the spike-filled cave on Dark World Death Mountain. Bring healing supplies and enough magic.

A hint for this chapter +

A green figure waits near the dark village.

Reveal the solution

Free the blacksmith transformed into a frog. Use the Magic Mirror to escort him to his Light World workshop, then have your sword tempered.

13

DARK WORLD · Crystal V · think from one floor to another.

The Ice Palace

Before you set outTitan's Mitt · Flippers · Fire Rod · Hammer · Hookshot

RewardsBlue Mail and fifth crystal

  1. On the central island of Lake Hylia in the Light World, lift the black rock. The teleporter drops you off in front of the dark entrance.

  2. Use the Fire Rod on the frozen guardian at the entrance. Take the key obtained by eliminating the following enemies.

  3. Set the crystal, plant a bomb near it then pass the barrier before the explosion. Bomb the cracked ground and fall.

  4. Hit each Stalfos to collapse it, then bombard its bones. Go down the corridors, collecting the keys.

  5. Follow the Hookshot route and stairs. At the mole-shaped obstacles, use the Hammer and glove, pull the statue’s tongue, then climb to the Big Key.

  6. Return to the room with a bombable floor beside the stairs. Bomb the floor and drop through to reach the big treasure chest containing the Blue Mail.

  7. Open the crystal passages then go around to the east and south to return to the left of the hole. Push the bottom block into it.

  8. Jump after the block, place it on the button and go through the door. Move the statue on the left, hammer the obstacles and lift the block that hides the final hole.

  9. Save magic for his shell. After the fight, take heart and fifth crystal.

A hint for this chapter +

The useful block must fall on the correct side of the hole.

Reveal the solution

On SNES, follow the dungeon’s looping route to reach the left side of the hole on the upper floor. Push the lower block into the hole, jump after it and place it on the floor switch. The Cane of Somaria offers a shortcut if you cleared Misery Mire first.

THE BOSS Kholdstare ↗

Melt the ice prison with the Fire Rod or Bombos. Then fight the three eyes while avoiding their contact and the shards that fall from the ceiling.

DARK WORLD · Crystal VI · give weight to magic.

Misery Mire

Before you set outActivated Flute · Titan's Mitt · Ether · Pegasus Boots · Fire Rod

RewardsCane of Somaria and sixth crystal

  1. In the Light World, use the Flute to fly to location 6. Lift the dark rock on the plateau to uncover the warp tile.

  2. Stand on the Ether symbol outside Misery Mire in the Dark World. Use the Ether Medallion with your sword equipped to open the entrance.

  3. Use the boots or the Hookshot to get through the opening. Eliminate the enemies needed to open the first doors.

  4. Collect the keys under the skulls and in the chests. Locate the floors connected by grids and the crystals that control the barriers.

  5. In the two neighboring blocky rooms, clear the torches and light all four quickly. Return to the transformed floor and fall down to the Big Key.

  6. Reach the big treasure chest in the eastern rooms: Hookshot across to the block, then dash along the collapsing walkway with the Pegasus Boots. Take the Cane of Somaria.

  7. Place a Somaria block on the button that does not stay pressed. In the dark rooms, bomb the north wall and activate the crystals from a distance.

  8. Gain the big door then beat Vitreous. Take the rewards before leaving.

A hint for this chapter +

The sound of a room changing is a clue of return.

Reveal the solution

Quickly light the four torches spread across the two neighboring rooms. Return to the displaced room and fall through the hole to reach the Big Key.

THE BOSS Vitreous ↗

Stay in a lower corner when lightning strikes in the center. Repel the little eyes with the sword; when the big eye pounces, use the bow or sword.

15

DARK WORLD · Turn preparation into power.

The Pyramid Fairy

Before you set outCrystals from Ice Palace and Misery Mire · Tempered Sword

RewardsGolden Sword and Silver Arrows

  1. After collecting crystals 5 and 6, visit the Bomb Shop at the Dark World counterpart of Link’s house.

  2. Buy the Super Bomb. It follows you: do not trigger it before reaching the cracked wall.

  3. Walk along the paths with the Super Bomb following you. Do not dash, jump off ledges or use travel methods that leave it behind. Bring it to the large crack in the Pyramid wall.

  4. Let the bomb explode against the cracked wall, then enter the Great Fairy Fountain.

  5. Throw your bow and Tempered Sword into the pool, one item at a time. Collect Silver Arrows and Golden Sword.

  6. Refill the bottles and restore the magic. Keep the Ice Rod in your preparation for the next dungeon.

A hint for this chapter +

Transporting the bomb is as important as purchasing it.

Reveal the solution

Buy the Super Bomb in Link's dark house, then walk to the Pyramid Crack without running or jumping off a ledge.

16

DARK WORLD · Crystal VII · fire, ice and hanging rails.

Turtle Rock

Before you set outTitan's Mitt · Hammer · Quake · Somaria · Fire Rod and Ice Rod

RewardsMirror Shield and last crystal

  1. On the eastern summit in the Light World, lift the dark rock. Hammer the three pegs in this order: right, top, left. Step onto the warp tile.

  2. On the Dark Summit emblem, launch Quake. Drop down past the turtle head and enter.

  3. Use Somaria on the question mark in the first gap. At intersections, choose your direction to reach each branch.

  4. In the room of four torches, prepare the Fire Rod before the circuit. Return to the door while all the flames are lit.

  5. Defeat the cacti for their keys. In the Chomp room, activate the crystal and push the upper block of the group on the left to reveal a chest. At the lava room, take the eastern pipe. Eliminate the cactus in the crystal room, open the east door by going backwards to avoid the laser, then enter the pipe: it leads to the Big Key.

  6. Continue to the two exits on the mountainside. The Mirror here allows a Light World detour for a Piece of Heart.

  7. Enter via the other access to the ledge, cross the gap with the Hookshot and open the large chest. Now orient the shield towards the lasers.

  8. On the rails, first reach the central island and its button under a skull. Take a platform again to reach the southwest exit.

  9. Cross the laser hallway with the Mirror Shield facing each eye. Collect the key in the last treasure chest, then pass the crystal-switch barriers to reach the boss.

  10. Defeat Trinexx, take heart and crystal. The seven girls can now open Ganon's Tower.

A hint for this chapter +

Red magic transforms certain points into platforms.

Reveal the solution

Use Somaria on question marks. Save enough magic to light the torches from the rails and for the boss's two elemental heads.

THE BOSS Trinexx ↗

Ice against the red head, fire against the blue, then sword during the stun. Destroy the blue head early to limit frozen ground. In its second form, hit the central glowing segment.

17

DARK WORLD · The tests are answered one last time.

Ganon’s Tower

Before you set outSeven crystals · Hookshot · Somaria · Fire · Bombs · Bow

RewardsRed Mail and access to Ganon

  1. At the top of the Dark Mountain, stand in front of the tower with the seven crystals.

  2. Take the keys, use Hammer and Hookshot to connect the platforms. Set the crystals with delayed bombs.

  3. Use a Somaria block to hold down the floor switch. Survive the flying tiles until the room unlocks. Continue through the torch and conveyor-belt puzzles.

  4. Use Somaria to explore invisible paths; Ether can reveal them briefly. Move forward in small segments.

  5. Reach the Armos Knights’ icy arena. Defeat them, then open the treasure chests in the next room to obtain the Big Key.

  6. Return to the large chest before going up. The tunic reduces damage from the last run.

  7. Go through the large doors and eliminate the waves of enemies; manage lasers and moving floors.

  8. Quickly light the four torches when the floor collapses. In the next room, collect the key from the enemies.

  9. Throw a bomb down the conveyor belt towards the cracked wall; continue through the colored barriers.

  10. Strike Moldorm’s tail without falling. Use the Hookshot on the newly revealed treasure chest, then climb the final stairs.

  11. Defeat Agahnim II. The scene takes you towards the Pyramid, but you can still resupply before Ganon.

A hint for this chapter +

Look for the treasure and the Big Key before going up.

Reveal the solution

The lower dungeon routes lead to the Armos Knights on ice and the Big Key. Return to the central big treasure chest before beginning the climb.

THE BOSS Agahnim II ↗

DARK WORLD · Two torches, an opening, a silver arrow.

The Final Battle

Before you set outSilver Arrows · Improved sword · Lantern or Fire · Magic

RewardsThe Triforce and the credits

  1. Before jumping into the Pyramid, refill your healing and keep a reserve of magic. Check the Silver Arrows in inventory.

  2. Strike Ganon with your sword when he stops moving. Dodge the trident instead of chasing him during a teleport.

  3. Move away from the circle of fire then dodge the bats. Stay away from the edges when the ground starts to disappear.

  4. Light both torches. While Ganon is visible, strike him with the sword to stun him, then immediately shoot a Silver Arrow. Relight the torches and repeat until he falls.

  5. After his defeat, take the northern passage. Watch the credits; Site tracking remains available to finish your collections.

A hint for this chapter +

In the last phase, the sword alone does not end the fight.

Reveal the solution

Relight the two torches, hit Ganon with your sword to immobilize him then immediately shoot a silver arrow. Repeat this cycle.

THE BOSS Ganon ↗

Avoid trident, fire bats and disappearing borders. The torches make Ganon visible and vulnerable in the last phase. A fall forces you to start the fight again.

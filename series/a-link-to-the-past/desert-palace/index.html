The key to this chapter

Obtain the Power Glove in the west wing, step out onto the ledge and reach the northern entrance behind the white rocks.

  1. South of Kakariko, hit the library shelf with the boots. Take the Book of Mudora.

  2. In the desert to the southwest, read the central inscription with the book. Enter when the passage opens.

  3. Explore the northwest of the first set. Run into the support holding a key to make it fall.

  4. Use this key to the east; eliminate the enemies and cross the corridor of projectiles to reach the Big Key.

  5. Return to the west. The switch hidden under a pot opens the large chest room: take the glove.

  6. Exit southwest; go down the ledge for the Piece of Heart. Go back up and lift the stones in front of the north entrance.

  7. Move between the rooms of flying tiles, taking the keys under the pots. In the last room, light the four torches.

  8. Defeat the three Lanmolas, then take the heart container and the pendant.

Lanmolas

Watch for moving sand. Strike the heads as they emerge, away from diagonal debris. The last worm throws more stones: give it space.

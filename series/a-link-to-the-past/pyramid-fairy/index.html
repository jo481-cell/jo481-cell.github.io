The key to this chapter

Buy the Super Bomb in Link's dark house, then walk to the Pyramid Crack without running or jumping off a ledge.

Before you set outCrystals from Ice Palace and Misery Mire · Tempered Sword

RewardsGolden Sword and Silver Arrows

Step-by-step walkthrough

  1. Make the Super Bomb appear
  2. Provide 100 rupees
  3. Walk towards the Pyramid
  4. Open the secret pool
  5. Upgrade Weapons
  6. Refill your supplies

  1. After collecting crystals 5 and 6, visit the Bomb Shop at the Dark World counterpart of Link’s house.

  2. Buy the Super Bomb. It follows you: do not trigger it before reaching the cracked wall.

  3. Walk along the paths with the Super Bomb following you. Do not dash, jump off ledges or use travel methods that leave it behind. Bring it to the large crack in the Pyramid wall.

  4. Let the bomb explode against the cracked wall, then enter the Great Fairy Fountain.

  5. Throw your bow and Tempered Sword into the pool, one item at a time. Collect Silver Arrows and Golden Sword.

  6. Refill the bottles and restore the magic. Keep the Ice Rod in your preparation for the next dungeon.

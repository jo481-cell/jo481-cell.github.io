01

Eastern Palace

0 / 3 steps+

Unlock wall merging.

Route and puzzle solutions

  1. 01

    Follow the sword delivery to the Sanctuary, then return home after Yuga's attack. Borrow Ravio's Bow and activate the paired switches outside Eastern Palace.

  2. 02

    Watch the rolling boulders before crossing their lanes. Shoot distant switches and ride the moving platforms to reach Yuga.

  3. 03

    Stun Yuga with arrows and close in with the sword. After gaining the bracelet's wall-merging power, use it to escape the dungeon along ledges you could not previously cross.

A useful detail

02

House of Gales

0 / 3 steps+

Earn another pendant.

Route and puzzle solutions

  1. 01

    Recover Queen Oren's Smooth Gem to obtain the Flippers, then rent the Tornado Rod. Swim to the House of Gales and use the rod at its wind-operated entrance.

  2. 02

    Use upward gusts and the Tornado Rod to reach moving platforms. Merge into walls around obstacles and keep enough energy to complete each crossing.

  3. 03

    Use the rod to rise above Margomill's rotating layers and strike the eye. Remove intervening layers before trying to reach the weak point again.

Chapter references
  • House of Gales ↗

Checkpoints

The encounter

Use Margomill's moving tiers to reach and strike its eye.

A useful detail

The Rod lifts vertically; it is not a directional jump.

03

Tower of Hera

0 / 3 steps+

Complete the pendants.

Route and puzzle solutions

  1. 01

    Obtain the Power Glove from Rosso and rent the Hammer. Climb Death Mountain and flatten the entrance pegs.

  2. 02

    Hit spring platforms with the Hammer, stand on them and wait for the launch. Use wall merging to move between ledges without falling to a lower floor.

  3. 03

    Moldorm's tail is vulnerable; its head is not. Fight near the centre and circle behind it rather than following it toward the edge.

Chapter references
  • Tower of Hera ↗

Checkpoints

The encounter

Strike Moldorm's tail, staying away from edges.

A useful detail

A fall may return you to a lower floor.

04

Master Sword and Hyrule Castle

0 / 3 steps+

Enter Lorule.

Route and puzzle solutions

  1. 01

    In the Lost Woods, follow the ghosts' changing instructions rather than assuming the same rule applies to every round. Draw the Master Sword with all three pendants.

  2. 02

    Break the castle barrier and climb to Yuga. Identify the real target among the copies and attack when he leaves the wall.

  3. 03

    Follow the newly opened passage to Lorule. Activate weather vanes and inspect world cracks: entering a crack from a different position can provide access to a different region.

05

Thieves' Hideout

0 / 3 steps+

Free Osfala.

Route and puzzle solutions

  1. 01

    Enter the hideout in Thieves' Town and free the imprisoned thief. Leave her in safety when scouting a dangerous room.

  2. 02

    Coordinate pressure switches, moving floors and barred passages. Merge into walls to reach controls while the thief waits, then return for her before proceeding.

  3. 03

    When Stalblind presents his shield, merge into it and emerge behind him. After rescuing Osfala, return to Ravio to obtain the Sand Rod for Desert Palace.

Chapter references
  • Thieves' Hideout ↗

Checkpoints

The encounter

Merge into Stalblind's shield, then emerge behind him to attack.

A useful detail

Keep enemies away from your companion.

06

Desert Palace

0 / 3 steps+

Rescue Irene.

Route and puzzle solutions

  1. 01

    Use Lorule's cracks to reach Hyrule's desert. Raise sand pillars with the Sand Rod and merge along their sides to cross gaps.

  2. 02

    Collect the Titan's Mitt and remove the large rocks blocking later routes. Keep track of which exits lead back outside rather than deeper into the palace.

  3. 03

    The boss lies across the route into Lorule. Raise a path toward Zaganaga with sand pillars, avoid the summoned enemies and slash the flower when within reach.

Chapter references
  • Desert Palace ↗

Checkpoints

The encounter

Build a route toward Zaganaga and strike between its attacks.

A useful detail

Sand columns collapse; plan the next jump.

07

Dark Palace

0 / 3 steps+

Rescue Gulley.

Route and puzzle solutions

  1. 01

    Sneak around the guards outside the palace, merging into walls to avoid their sightlines. Rent Bombs before entering.

  2. 02

    Break boarded windows to send light onto sensors. In other rooms, extinguish torches to reveal paths that are only visible in darkness.

  3. 03

    Bomb the Gemasaur King's mask to expose its weak point. Relight the room when darkness hides it, then strike while avoiding its charges.

Chapter references
  • Dark Palace ↗

Checkpoints

The encounter

Bomb Gemasaur King's helmet, strike its exposed spot and relight the torches.

A useful detail

Keep the Lantern equipped for the boss.

08

Swamp Palace

0 / 3 steps+

Rescue Oren.

Route and puzzle solutions

  1. 01

    Rent the Hookshot and bring the large Bomb Flower to the cracked boulder near the entrance. Its explosion opens the route and changes the surrounding water.

  2. 02

    Pull switches and adjust water levels to reach different ledges. Use the Hookshot on targets across gaps before raising water that changes access.

  3. 03

    Pull Arrghus's smaller eyes away one at a time and destroy them. Once the shield is gone, evade the main body's jumps and attack when it lands.

Chapter references
  • Swamp Palace ↗

Checkpoints

The encounter

Pull Arrghus's small eyes with the Hookshot, destroy them and attack its body.

A useful detail

Ordinary bombs cannot remove that boulder.

09

Skull Woods

0 / 3 steps+

Rescue Seres.

Route and puzzle solutions

  1. 01

    Use the forest's holes as separate dungeon entrances. Mark which upper opening leads to each lower room rather than repeatedly dropping into the same branch.

  2. 02

    Carry the eyeballs to their sockets, using wall merging and moving platforms to reconnect paths. A carried eye cannot follow every route that Link can traverse alone.

  3. 03

    When Knucklemaster charges, merge into the wall and let it crash. Emerge to attack during the recovery, then move before its next attempt.

Chapter references
  • Skull Woods ↗

Checkpoints

The encounter

Merge when Knucklemaster charges; attack after it hits the wall.

A useful detail

Watch the shadow of falling hands.

10

Turtle Rock

0 / 3 steps+

Rescue Impa.

Route and puzzle solutions

  1. 01

    Help the three baby turtles and reunite them with their mother to reach the lake entrance. Bring the Ice Rod.

  2. 02

    Freeze lava and moving seesaw platforms to create stable crossings. Merge along accessible walls when a frozen platform alone does not reach the exit.

  3. 03

    Drop ice through the floor openings onto Grinexx below. When it surfaces, move away from its attacks and strike the exposed head.

Chapter references
  • Turtle Rock ↗

Checkpoints

The encounter

Drop ice onto Grinexx through the floor openings, then strike its head when it surfaces.

A useful detail

Ice platforms are temporary.

11

Ice Ruins

0 / 3 steps+

Rescue Rosso.

Route and puzzle solutions

  1. 01

    Reach the mountain's Lorule crack with the Fire Rod available. Melt the entrance and pull the central statue's tongue to operate the lift.

  2. 02

    Treat the dungeon as a controlled descent: inspect where a hole lands before dropping, melt obstructing ice and pull tongue switches to move floors into place.

  3. 03

    Use repeated Fire Rod attacks to melt Dharkstare's shell. Slash the exposed creature and leave the marked triangular areas before they freeze.

Chapter references
  • Ice Ruins ↗

Checkpoints

The encounter

Melt Dharkstare's ice coat, slash it and avoid its triangular freezing zones.

A useful detail

The central lift reconnects explored levels.

12

Lorule Castle

0 / 3 steps+

Restore both kingdoms.

Route and puzzle solutions

  1. 01

    After rescuing all seven sages, complete Lorule Castle's four trials. Use the tools and wall-merging techniques tested in the earlier dungeons.

  2. 02

    Against Yuga Ganon, evade the trident attacks and return the energy volleys with the sword. Keep room to move rather than standing at the arena edge.

  3. 03

    Use Zelda's Bow of Light during the wall phases. Shoot from behind; in the final variation, fire along the wall in the opposite direction so the arrow travels around to Yuga's back.

Chapter references
  • Lorule Castle ↗

Checkpoints

The encounter

Shoot Yuga from behind; for the final variation, fire around the wall in the opposite direction so the arrow reaches his back.

A useful detail

The Bow of Light fires while you are merged.

Collection field guide

Plan your final discoveries.

Use these routes alongside the edition-specific counters. The references link to individual locations and additional maps.

Maiamais and equipment improvements

  1. 01

    Visit Mother Maiamai to begin the search. Listen for the cries, use wall merging to dislodge wall-bound Maiamais and revisit rocks and deep water with upgraded equipment.

  2. 02

    Buy an item from Ravio before asking Mother Maiamai to improve it. Rentals cannot receive those permanent upgrades.

  3. 03

    Collect the four Master Ore pieces and take the pairs to the appropriate blacksmiths. Search the bottle locations separately; an empty bottle is a permanent reward even if its contents change.

Edition notes

Lorule permits several dungeon orders. This itinerary places Thieves' Hideout before Desert Palace because rescuing Osfala makes the Sand Rod available from Ravio.

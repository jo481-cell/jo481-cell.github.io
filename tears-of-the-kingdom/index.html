Before you set out

Your journey, at your pace.

Switch campaign using standard abilities, without glitches or version-dependent shortcuts.

  • Fuse weapons to strengthen them.
  • Bring Brightblooms for darkness and Sundelion meals for gloom-damaged hearts.
  • Activate travel points.

Riju and the Lightning Temple

The encounter

Strike Queen Gibdo with lightning, then attack; destroy glowing nests when their protective covering opens.

Elemental attacks make Gibdos vulnerable to ordinary weapons.

Beneath Hyrule Castle

0 / 3 steps+

Defeat Ganondorf.

The encounter

Dodge Ganondorf's counters after he evades your strike. Destroy the Demon Dragon's back growths, then strike its head's stone.

A useful detail

Cook before descending; gloom removes usable hearts.

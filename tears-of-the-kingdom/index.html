2023 / A world above and below

Tears of the Kingdom

Look up. Then look beneath your feet. Hyrule has become a place to build, experiment and discover again. Here is a starting point for your next journey.

← All gamesLet the Great Sky Island teach youFour ways to rethink a problemFind your footing at Lookout LandingMaps & detailed guides
01

Let the Great Sky Island teach you

The opening island introduces Link’s new abilities through its shrines and traversal challenges. Work through the opening quest before heading down to the surface. After landing, Lookout Landing provides the main story’s next point of contact.

  • Experiment with the materials placed near each early puzzle.
  • Keep food and watch your temperature before crossing snowy ground.
  • Return to the current quest objective when you lose the thread.
02

Four ways to rethink a problem

Ultrahand moves and connects objects. Fuse combines materials with equipment. Ascend takes Link through suitable ceilings above him. Recall reverses an object’s recent motion. Before searching for one intended answer, consider what each ability changes about the obstacle.

  • Try a small construction before making it complicated.
  • Look upward when a wall seems to block your route.
  • Consider an object’s movement as well as its position.
03

Find your footing at Lookout Landing

Speak to Purah and follow the early surface objectives. The Regional Phenomena quest gives you leads across Hyrule, while the world remains open to exploration. Treat the sky, surface and depths as connected spaces, and keep track of places you want to revisit.

  • Prepare supplies before a long expedition away from a settlement.
  • Use map markers to separate a discovery from a task for later.
  • Consult a detailed guide only for the obstacle you want help with.

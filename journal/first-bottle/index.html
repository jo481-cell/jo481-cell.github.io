Keep a little mystery.

Start with the gentlest clue, or go straight to the route below.

01A gentle nudge+

Someone you helped near the castle has returned to work. Visit the ranch in the middle of Hyrule Field.

02A clearer direction+

The first building on the left holds a game with a useful prize. Watch which three birds enter the room last.

03The final clue+

You can tidy the room before starting: move the ordinary Cuccos behind the table so the three newcomers are easier to follow.

Ready when you areThe complete route+

Win Talon’s Super Cucco game: pay 10 Rupees, then pick up his three special Cuccos within 30 seconds. Your first victory gives you a bottle filled with milk.

  1. Find Talon at home

    After waking him with the hatched chicken at Hyrule Castle, visit Lon Lon Ranch in daylight. Enter the first building to your left.

  2. Make the room easier to read

    Before talking to Talon, carry the ordinary Cuccos behind the table. This optional preparation helps separate them from the three he releases.

  3. Catch the three Super Cuccos

    Pay for the game and watch the birds land. Pick them up; Talon confirms each correct one. Find all three before the 30-second countdown expires.

  4. Keep the container

    Collect the milk bottle. Drinking its two servings leaves an empty bottle you can reuse. Repeating the game supplies milk rather than another new bottle.

It does not have to be your first

The name describes a convenient early option, not a required collection order. Kakariko’s Cucco rescue is another route to an early empty bottle.

A failed attempt is recoverable

Talon offers a retry for 5 Rupees after a loss. There is no need to reload a save merely because the timer ran out.

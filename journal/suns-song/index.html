Ready when you areThe complete route+

Play Zelda’s Lullaby at the Royal Family’s Tomb in Kakariko Graveyard. Defeat the Keese, pass the ReDeads and inspect the final inscription to learn the Sun’s Song.

  1. Open the royal tomb

    Walk to the large tombstone at the back of the Graveyard. Stand on the Triforce symbol and play Zelda’s Lullaby. Drop through the opening revealed by the lightning.

  2. Clear the first room

    Defeat all five Keese to release the door. The Slingshot makes this easier from a distance. Continue up the steps.

  3. Cross without a fight

    The next chamber contains ReDeads and harmful green pools. Keep to safe ground and give the ReDeads space; defeating them is not required to reach the inscription.

  4. Read, then return

    Check the stone inscription in the final chamber to learn the song. Play it on the return journey to stun the ReDeads, then use the entrance’s return pad.

A practical reward

The Sun’s Song changes day to night or night to day in suitable outdoor areas. It can also temporarily freeze ReDeads, giving you room to move.

N64 buttons

Play C-Right, C-Down, C-Up, then repeat. Learning the melody in the tomb matters; knowing the button sequence alone does not unlock it.

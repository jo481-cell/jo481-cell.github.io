02Urban legend

Always one second too late

You cut every corner and cross the bridge faster than before. He is already there. What if the missing second was never yours to win?

Reveal the verdict +
No normal-play victory

In the original game’s normal race, the Running Man reports finishing one second faster than your time. A better route does not unlock a victory reward.

03Cut content & folklore

The fountain behind the wall

An unfamiliar fountain in an early image became a destination that players hoped was still waiting behind a sealed wall.

Reveal the verdict +
Pre-release image, unproven route

The fan-named Unicorn Fountain appears in pre-release material, but it is not a discoverable location in the completed original game. Claims about a hidden entrance or promised reward are a separate layer of rumour.

And some discoveries were real

Small secrets. Real rewards.

Verified mechanic · Original game

The sword that really does last

The Giant’s Knife and Biggoron’s Sword look related, but they are not interchangeable rewards.

Reveal how it works +

Biggoron’s Sword is the reward for the optional adult trading sequence and does not break. Buying the Giant’s Knife is not required to complete that quest.

Verified mechanic · Original game

A tune that belongs to you

Not every useful song is handed to Link. One optional melody begins with the notes you choose.

Reveal how it works +

Teach an eight-note tune to Bonooru at Lake Hylia as a child, then play it for him again as an adult. The Scarecrow’s Song can call Pierre at designated locations, creating a Hookshot target.

Chapter 01

Release and platform

The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time is scheduled for November 5, 2026 and is listed as exclusive to Nintendo Switch 2.

  • Release date: November 5, 2026
  • Platform: Nintendo Switch 2
  • Publisher: Nintendo

Chapter 02

Rebuilt presentation

Nintendo describes the game as built from the ground up. Its product page confirms completely overhauled visuals, voiced cutscenes, expanded dialogue and symphonic orchestral music.

  • Voiced cutscenes
  • Expanded dialogue
  • Symphonic orchestral music
  • Completely overhauled visuals

Chapter 03

Movement and camera

Nintendo says players can jump and dash with enhanced controls. The game also uses modern camera movements, a substantial change in feel from the fixed conventions of the 1998 release.

  • Jump and dash actions
  • Enhanced controls
  • Modern camera movement

Chapter 04

Technical listing

The Nintendo product page currently estimates a 27 GB file size. It lists one player on a single system, Save Data Cloud support, HDR output and VRR output on compatible displays.

Nintendo notes that details such as ratings and download size may not be final before release. This archive will update the record if the official listing changes.
